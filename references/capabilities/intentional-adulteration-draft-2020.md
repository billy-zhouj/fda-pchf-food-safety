**② 纠正措施：书面程序、两层目标、与食品安全纠正措施的差异**：

> "You must establish and implement written food defense corrective actions procedures that must be taken if mitigation strategies are not properly implemented (121.145(a)(1))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.5.A [pdf-page 4]；法条在〔§ 121.145(a)(1)〕；原文此处未写 `21 CFR`，不得径自补全。

> "The food defense corrective actions procedures must describe the corrective actions steps you would take to ensure that appropriate action is taken to identify and correct a problem that has occurred with implementation of a mitigation strategy (21 CFR 121.145(a)(2)(i)) and, when necessary, to reduce the likelihood that the problem will recur (21 CFR 121.145(a)(2)(ii))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.5.A [pdf-page 4]；两层目标：已纠正 + 降低复发；只写第一层不完整。

> "your corrective actions procedures do not need to ensure that all affected food is evaluated for safety and that all affected food is prevented from entering into commerce if it cannot be ensured that the affected food is not adulterated or misbranded."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.5.B [pdf-page 5]；**与 PCHF 最关键的分流**：不承担〔§ 117.150(a)(2)(iii)(iv)〕的评估全部受影响食品与阻止进入商业的义务。

> "Corrective actions are taken after food defense monitoring or verification determines that a mitigation strategy is not operating as intended."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.5.C [pdf-page 5]；启动点是 monitoring **或** verification 判定策略未按预期运行。

> "The need for such reporting is typically addressed in food defense awareness training."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.5.D [pdf-page 7]；怀疑食品被污染或 root cause 无法确定时的上报要求，通常在意识培训中讲授。

> "Food defense corrective actions records must include a description of the steps taken to identify and correct the problem with implementation of the mitigation strategy. (21 CFR 121.145(b))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.5.E [pdf-page 8]；纠正记录须含问题描述与所采取步骤，法条在〔§ 121.145(b)〕。


**③ 验证：四类法定活动与「其他验证活动」的书面程序**：

> "Food defense verification is the application of methods, procedures, and other evaluations, in addition to food defense monitoring, to determine whether a mitigation strategy or combination of mitigation strategies is or has been operating as intended according to the food defense plan. (21 CFR 121.3)."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.6.A [pdf-page 14]；验证定义，法源〔§ 121.3〕。

> "You must verify that food defense monitoring is being conducted. (21 CFR 121.150(a)(1)). You have flexibility to determine how you verify food defense monitoring is being conducted, how frequently you do so, and who conducts this activity."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.6.C.1 [pdf-page 15]；验证活动①：监控是否实施；方式、频次、执行人自选。

> "You must verify that appropriate decisions about food defense corrective actions are being made. (21 CFR 121.150(a)(2)). You have flexibility to determine how you verify that appropriate decisions about food defense corrective actions are being made, how frequently you do so, and who conducts this activity."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.6.C.2 [pdf-page 16]；验证活动②：纠正决策是否适当。

> "You must verify that mitigation strategies are properly implemented and are significantly minimizing or preventing the significant vulnerabilities. (21 CFR 121.150(a)(3))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.6.C.3 [pdf-page 17]；验证活动③：策略是否正确实施并显著最小化/预防；本项要求最高，须记录审查 + 其他适当活动。

> "If you conduct other verification activities, you must have written procedures for them, including the frequency for which they are to be performed, in your FDP. (21 CFR 121.150(b))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.6.C.3.f [pdf-page 21]；(a)(3)(ii) 其他验证活动须有**含频次**的书面程序并写进 FDP——最常漏的一条。

> "You must verify that reanalysis of your FDP occurred when required by 21 CFR 121.157. (21 CFR 121.150(a)(4))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.6.C.4 [pdf-page 22]；验证活动④：验证再分析已按〔§ 121.157〕完成。


**④ 再分析：1 个周期 + 4 类情境、部分再分析不重启时钟、90 日与书面理由**：

> "The purpose of the reanalysis is to determine whether your FDP continues to be current and accurately reflects your significant vulnerabilities and to determine whether your mitigation strategies and mitigation strategy management components remain appropriate for your facility."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.7.A [pdf-page 30]；再分析的目的：计划是否现行准确、策略与管理组件是否仍适当。

> "You must conduct a reanalysis of your FDP as a whole at least once every 3 years. (21 CFR 121.157(a))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.7.A [pdf-page 30]；整份计划至少每 3 年一次；是「至少」，事件触发另行叠加。

> "(4) FDA requires reanalysis to respond to new vulnerabilities, credible threats to the food supply, and developments in scientific understanding including, as appropriate, results from the Department of Homeland Security biological, chemical, radiological, or other terrorism risk assessment (21 CFR 121.157(b)(4))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.7.A [pdf-page 30]；第四类触发是 FDA 要求，含 DHS 风险评估结果。

> "Note that a reanalysis that does not include the entire FDP does not restart the three-year time period."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.7.B.1 [pdf-page 31]；**部分再分析不重启 3 年时钟**；下一次整体再分析仍须涵盖之。

> "You must complete your reanalysis and implement any necessary additional mitigation strategies before changes in activities become operative and, when necessary, within 90 calendar days after production."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.7.E [pdf-page 35]；变更类须在**生效前**完成，生产后情形必要时 90 日历日内完成。

> "A time period exceeding 90 days after production of the applicable food first begins is permissible if the time period is reasonable and a written justification is prepared. (21 CFR 121.157(c))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.7.E [pdf-page 35]；超 90 日不是自动允许，须合理且备书面理由。

> "If you conduct a reanalysis and determine that no revisions are needed, you must document the basis for this conclusion (i.e., an explanation for why there were no changes needed). (21 CFR 121.157(d))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.7.F [pdf-page 36]；结论「无需修订」也要书面记录依据。


**⑤ 记录：通用要求、同步创建、监控附加、两种 2 年、异地 24 小时、既有记录复用**：

> "Your required records must be accurate, legible, and indelible. (21 CFR 121.305(c))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.9.B.2 [pdf-page 38]；记录须准确、清晰、不可擦。

> "Your required records must be created concurrently with performance of the activity documented. (21 CFR 121.305(d))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.9.B.3 [pdf-page 38]；**同步创建**，不得事后补记。

> "In addition to the requirements described in Section B above, your food defense monitoring records must contain the actual values and observations obtained during the monitoring. (21 CFR 121.305(b))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.9.C [pdf-page 40]；监控记录须含实际数值与观察结果，不能只写模糊记号。

> "You are required to retain records for at least 2 years after they were prepared. (21 CFR 121.315(a)(1)). You must retain a food defense plan for 2 years after you have stopped using it. (21 CFR 121.315(b))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.9.E [pdf-page 41]；两种「2 年」对象不同：一般记录自制备起、计划自**停用后**。

> "Note that the FDP must remain onsite. (21 CFR 121.315(c)). Electronic records, including the FDP, are considered onsite if they are accessible from an onsite location. (21 CFR 121.315(c))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.9.F [pdf-page 42]；计划必须留现场；电子记录可在现场访问即视为现场。

> "If your facility is closed for a prolonged period, you may transfer the FDP to some other reasonably accessible location but you must be able to retrieve it and make it available within 24 hours of an FDA request. (21 CFR 121.315(d))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.9.F [pdf-page 42]；长期停业转移后仍须 24 小时内可调取。

> "You may use existing records kept for other purposes (e.g., records that you have already developed and maintain during your normal course of business, including for compliance with other Federal, state, or local regulations, or for any other reason) to meet the requirements of the IA rule, if those records contain all of the required information and satisfy all of the requirements of the IA rule. (21 CFR 121.330(a))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance Ch.9.G [pdf-page 42]；既有记录可复用，但须含全部要求信息；食品安全记录复用须在 FDP 中说明。


**⑥ FDMSD：自愿使用的缓解策略数据库与分类结构（转述为主，Table 1/2 不可逐字引）**：

> "FDA's online Food Defense Mitigation Strategies Database (FDMSD) contains a collection of potential mitigation strategies that could be implemented to significantly minimize or prevent significant vulnerabilities at actionable process steps."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance App.2 [pdf-page 44]；FDMSD 是在线数据库，不是强制清单。

> "The FDMSD is intended as a starting point for facilities to consider when identifying potential mitigation strategies. Facilities can customize and tailor strategies listed in the FDMSD to apply to their specific circumstances. Use of the FDMSD is voluntary."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance App.2 [pdf-page 44]；`voluntary` 明写；可定制，不是照抄即合规。

> "The mitigation strategies within the FDMSD are organized by category, subcategory (if applicable), and points, steps, or procedures."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance App.2 Organization [pdf-page 44]；分类结构：category → subcategory → points/steps/procedures。

> "There are no points, steps, or procedures under the Key Activity Types (KAT) category."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance App.2 Organization [pdf-page 45]；FDMSD 的 KAT 类下**无** points/steps/procedures，须回到 CAP-37 Ch.2.D 看四类 KAT。


**⑦ 极小企业/小企业判定：3 年均值、含未售市值、排除动物食品、计算示例**：

> "The IA requirements do not apply to a very small business, except that a very small business must, upon request, provide for official review documentation sufficient to show that the facility meets the criteria for the exemption; such documentation must be retained for 2 years. (21 CFR 121.5(a))."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance App.3.I [pdf-page 54]；举证式豁免，支撑材料 2 年。

> "small business means, for purposes of the IA rule, a business (including any subsidiaries and affiliates) averaging less than $10,000,000, adjusted for inflation, per year, during the 3-year period preceding the applicable calendar year in sales of human food plus the market value of human food manufactured, processed, packed, or held without sale (e.g., held for a fee)."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance App.3.A.1 [pdf-page 54]；原文此处低格写 `small business means`，指的却是 very small business 定义〔§ 121.3〕；不得照字面误读为 500 FTE 的小企业。

> "The definition of a very small business (21 CFR 121.3) specifies that the average is based on the three-year period preceding the applicable calendar year. The applicable calendar year is the current year."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance App.3.B.4 [pdf-page 57]；3 年窗口随当年滚动，本年度为 applicable calendar year。

> "Use the value of the food, not the fee for the service (e.g., for holding, processing, or packing), to calculate the market value of food that you manufacture, process, pack or hold without sale."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance App.3.B.7 [pdf-page 59]；未销售食品市值按**食品价值**而非服务费计算。

> "Warehouse N does not meet the definition of a very small business because the three-year average value of $13,510,000 is greater than $11,011,028 (inflation adjusted value of $10,000,000 in 2018)."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance App.3.C.1 [pdf-page 61]；FDA 计算示例；$11,011,028 是 **2018 年**的通胀调整值，每年变化，须查 FDA 现行公布值。

> "The definition of a small business is "a business (including any subsidiaries and affiliates) employing fewer than 500 full-time equivalent employees" (21 CFR 121.3)."
> —— Supplemental Draft Guidance App.3.II [pdf-page 62]；small business 是 <500 FTE，与 <$10,000,000 的 very small business 是两套门槛。


## I — 自述

本卡把 CAP-32 的三件套法条〔§ 121.138–121.150〕、再分析〔§ 121.157〕与记录〔§ 121.301–121.330〕，按 FDA 2020-02 草案的操作口径展开；同时给出两个实用工具：FDMSD 缓解策略数据库与极小/小企业规模判定计算。它与上册 CAP-37 是「先判哪里脆弱、选什么策略」与「策略出错怎么办、怎么证明一直有效」的前后段关系。

**四组最容易反直觉的口径：**

1. **食品防御纠正措施 ≠ PCHF 纠正措施。** 本件明文说「不需要确保全部受影响食品被评估安全性并阻止进入商业」——因为 IA 事件需要「策略未正确实施的机会 + 有意图的内部攻击者」两个条件，多数实施失败不会导致掺假。把〔§ 117.150〕的义务搬进来是实质错误。
2. **penetration audit 一鱼三吃。** 采用渗透式审核可同时满足 (a)(1)(a)(2)(a)(3) 三类验证、免做记录审查，但**程序与频率必须书面写进 FDP**；反过来选记录审查时，把频率写进 FDP 只是 FDA 建议。
3. **再分析触发是「1 + 4」不是五个并列情境。** 3 年周期是一条独立时钟；部分再分析**不重启**它，下一次整体再分析仍须涵盖已部分分析的部分。90 日历日也不是绝对上限——合理且书面理由可超过。
4. **$10,000,000 与 $250,000 不是一回事。** Part 121 的 very small business 门槛是 $10,000,000（3 年均值、通胀调整）；$250,000 属 Parts 117/507 语境，本件全文零命中。$11,011,028 只是 2018 年通胀调整示例值，每年变。

## A1 — 判定场景

| 场景 | 回答要点 |
|---|---|
| 监控发现策略没按预期运行，要不要隔离产品 | 食品防御纠正措施不承担〔§ 117.150〕的「评估全部受影响食品安全性和阻止进入商业」义务；但怀疑食品被污染或 root cause 无法确定时须立即上报 |
| 纠正程序要写多细 | 覆盖最可能发生的失败情形即可，不必穷尽所有方式；FDA 预期多数程序「简单、易执行」 |
| 只写「已纠正」够不够 | 不够——还须在必要时写明降低复发可能性的措施 |
| 靠什么启动纠正措施 | 食品防御监控**或**验证判定策略未按预期运行；两条路径都可触发 |
| 验证有几类活动 | 四类：验证监控在实施、验证纠正决策适当、验证策略正确实施并显著最小化/预防、验证再分析已发生 |
| 用记录审查做验证够不够 | 对 (a)(3) 策略充分性验证，须记录审查**加**其他适当活动；只审记录不够 |
| 用 penetration audit 替代记录审查行不行 | 可以，且可同时满足 (a)(1)(a)(2)(a)(3)；但程序与频率必须书面写进 FDP |
| 用「其他验证活动」要注意什么 | 必须建立并实施书面程序，**含执行频率**，写进 FDP〔§ 121.150(b)〕 |
| 3 年再分析时钟怎么算 | 整体再分析重置时钟；部分再分析**不重置**；提前完成整体再分析则从完成日重新起算 |
| 新增一条独立产线，要重做多少 | 可只做部分再分析（新线相关部分）；但下次整体再分析须涵盖它 |
| 变更下个月生效，什么时候再分析 | 生效前完成；生产后发现的情形必要时 90 日历日内完成并实施新增策略 |
| 超过 90 天是否一定违法 | 不一定——期限合理且备有书面理由即可；突发设备故障另有〔§ 121.157(c)(3)〕路径 |
| 再分析结论「不用改」 | 仍须书面记录该结论的依据 |
| 记录能不能事后补 | 不能——须与活动**同步**创建，且准确、清晰、不可擦 |
| 监控记录只写「OK」行不行 | 不行——须含监控中获得的实际数值与观察结果 |
| 两种「2 年」分别指什么 | 一般记录自制备起 2 年；FDP 自**停用后**再 2 年；极小企业支撑记录另按适用日历年算 |
| 计划能存总部服务器吗 | FDP 必须现场；电子记录可从现场访问即视为现场；长期停业转移后仍须 24 小时内可调 |
| 已有 HACCP/清洗记录能不能用 | 可复用或补充，信息须覆盖全部要求；建议在 FDP 相应章节注明复用关系 |
| FDMSD 里的策略能照抄吗 | 不能——自愿使用、只是起点，须按本设施具体情形定制；FDA 不保证穷尽 |
| FDMSD 有几类、几条策略 | 七大类（Conveyance / Material Handling / Packaging / Processing / Storage / Transportation-Distribution / KAT），Table 2 列 42 条策略；KAT 类下无 points/steps/procedures |
| 年销售额九百万美元算极小企业吗 | 先取适用日历年前 3 年的销售额 + 未售人类食品市值，按通胀调整均值与 FDA 当年公布门槛比；含子公司与关联方、含出口与 USDA 肉制品，排除动物食品 |
| 代客仓储收的是服务费，按什么算市值 | 按**食品价值**算，不按服务费；可参考客户来料价值、保险额、市价×数量、资产负债表 |

## A2 — 未来触发

- 问「策略失效了要不要召回/拦截产品」→ 本卡只答食品防御纠正措施法条口径；产品处置与召回走 CAP-12／CAP-13，不得从本卡推导。
- 问「验证要不要做产品测试、环境监测、设备校准」→ 那是 PCHF validation/verification（CAP-02）；IA 验证是四类活动，不含这些。
- 问「KATs 怎么判、三要素怎么打分」→ 转 CAP-37；FDMSD 的七大类与 KATs 四类不可混答。
- 问「2017 SECG 的 Table 2 和本件 App.3 的 Table 2 是同一张吗」→ 不是。SECG Table 2 是豁免表；本件 App.2 Table 2 是策略×类别矩阵；App.3 内另有 Table 1–4 计算示例，至少四个「Table 2」不可混引。
- 问「$11,011,028 是现行门槛吗」→ 不是，是 2018 年通胀调整示例；现行门槛须查 FDA 网站。
- 问「现场检查会看哪些记录、怎么判不符合」→ 检查员侧转 CAP-16；本卡只给企业义务。
- 问「这份指南生效了吗」→ 它是 2020-02 草案（Draft—Not for Implementation），不是生效文件；现行义务回 CAP-32 法条与官方公告。
- 问「经济利益驱动的掺假适用本卡吗」→ 不属 food defense；走 CAP-06。

## E — 执行要点

**Step 1 — 先确认效力层再动笔。** 凡「必须/不得」回 CAP-32〔§ 121.xxx〕；本卡的操作细则全部标 `Supplemental Draft Guidance 章.节 [pdf-page N]` 并注明是未定稿草案；冲突时法条 > CAP-36 最终指南 > 两册草案。

**Step 2 — 纠正措施按两层目标写程序。** 纠正已发生问题 + 必要时降低复发；只覆盖最可能失败情形即可；不要搬入〔§ 117.150〕的全部受影响食品评估与拦截义务；怀疑污染或 root cause 不明时立即上报。

**Step 3 — 验证按四类活动逐项留痕。** (a)(1) 监控在实施、(a)(2) 纠正决策适当、(a)(3) 策略正确实施并显著最小化、(a)(4) 再分析已发生；(a)(3) 须记录审查 + 其他活动；用 (a)(3)(ii) 或其他验证活动须有**含频次**书面程序并写进 FDP。

**Step 4 — 再分析分轨计时。** 3 年整体时钟独立；部分再分析不重置；事件触发（重大变更 / 新信息 / 实施不当 / FDA 要求）另行叠加；变更生效前完成、生产后 90 日历日内、超期书面理由；「无需修订」也留痕。

**Step 5 — 六类记录分别建台账。** FDP、监控、纠正、验证、再分析、培训；通用八项要求逐项核；同步创建；监控记录含实际数值与观察结果。

**Step 6 — 保存与取回按对象分列。** 一般记录制备起 2 年、FDP 停用后 2 年、极小企业支撑记录按适用日历年；FDP 必须现场，其余可异地但 24 小时内取回；长期停业转移后仍须 24 小时可调。

**Step 7 — 复用既有记录要核信息完整。** HACCP/清洗/质量记录可复用或补充，但须含全部要求信息，并在 FDP 相应章节注明。

**Step 8 — FDMSD 只作起点。** 按 category → subcategory → points/steps/procedures 查，可定制；七大类与 KATs 四类分开；KAT 类下无 points/steps/procedures，须回 CAP-37。

**Step 9 — 规模判定按四步算。** 定 3 年窗口 → 逐年算销售额 + 未售人类食品市值（按食品价值非服务费） → 算均值 → 与 FDA 当年公布的通胀调整门槛比；含子公司/关联方、含出口与 USDA 肉制品，排除动物食品；500 FTE 是另一套 small business 分档。

**Step 10 — 收尾声明。** CARVER + Shock、Plan Builder、专项 CP、EMA、召回与产品处置一律「转引未收」。全包现为**三十类来源不可混引**、十二套法规编号；本卡不新增编号，只新增 FDA 指南第 9 类（草案子类第 2 份）。

## B — 边界

**B1 — 草案不是最终指南，更不是法条。** 封面明写 `Draft—Not for Implementation` 与 `comment purposes only`；本卡所有操作都是征求意见中的建议，不得当 FDA 现行定论，不得据此断言「合规」或「不合规」。法定义务只回 CAP-32。

**B2 — 本件是下册，不是完整指南。** 只补 Ch.5/6/7/9 与 App.2/3；KATs／三要素／Hybrid／策略选择／监控四问／培训分档在 CAP-37；两册合起来才覆盖 IA 指南草案全文。

**B3 — 承接关系不能逐字引 `coming soon`。** 本件全文无该词；「补齐上册 coming soon 章节」是由封面 Supplemental 定位 + 目录 + 反引上册章节推定的编辑性结论，须标为推断而非原文。

**B4 — 食品防御纠正措施 ≠ PCHF 纠正措施。** 本件明文排除〔§ 117.150(a)(2)(iii)(iv)〕的全部受影响食品评估与拦截义务；IA 事件须「实施失败机会 + 内部攻击者意图」并存。把 PCHF 纠正流程搬进来是实质错误。

**B5 — 验证比 PCHF 轻，但不等于可以不做。** 不含 validation、calibration、product testing、environmental monitoring；四类活动是〔§ 121.150〕的法定义务；(a)(3) 的记录审查 + 其他活动不可省。

**B6 — penetration audit 与记录审查二选一，但书面程序不可省。** 前者可同时满足 (a)(1)(a)(2)(a)(3) 并免记录审查；后者把频率写进 FDP 只是 FDA 建议，不是法定要求。

**B7 — 再分析是「1 + 4」，90 日不是绝对上限。** 部分再分析不重启 3 年时钟；下一次整体再分析须涵盖之；超 90 日须合理且备书面理由；突发设备故障另有〔§ 121.157(c)(3)〕。

**B8 — 两个「2 年」与三种起算点不可合并。** 一般记录自制备起、FDP 自停用后、极小企业支撑记录按适用日历年；加上 24 小时异地取回，共四套时限对象。

**B9 — 表与图的不可逐字引用区。** Ch.5 表 5-9～5-12、Ch.6 表 6.13～6.16、App.2 Table 1（7 类分类）与 Table 2（42 条 × 7 类 X 矩阵）、App.3 Table 1～4（计算示例）、Figure 1 组织结构图，均为多栏错行或图形标签混入正文；只可转述结构与数值。App.3 另有至少四个「Table 2」（本件 App.2 Table 2、App.3 内表、CAP-36 SECG Table 2 豁免表），不可混引。

**B10 — App.3 Table 3 公式行有原文数字不一致。** 表格列 $2,255,000，公式行却写 `($2,225,000)(6) = $13,530,000`（两者乘积不符）；均值 $13,510,000 本身正确。该公式行**禁止逐字引用**，只能转述「按保险额 × 周转次数估算市值」的方法。

**B11 — 规模判定的两个门槛不可混。** very small business 是 <$10,000,000（3 年均值、通胀调整、含未售人类食品市值）；small business 是 <500 FTE；$250,000 全文零命中，属 Parts 117/507 语境。$11,011,028 是 2018 年示例值，不是现行门槛。

**B12 — 引用规范（全包统一，三十类不可混引）。** 法规正文仍为十二套编号〔§ 1 / 101 / 108 / 113 / 114 / 117 / 120 / 121 / 123 / 172.xxx〕+〔§ 1.13xx/1.14xx〕+〔§ 1.9xx〕；FDA 最终指南为 should 建议层（CAP-36 第 7 类），**本件为草案建议层（第 9 类，最低）**，CPGM／CP／IOM 为检查员视角、不创设企业义务。**Part 1 三套 subpart（L / O / S）引用必须带 subpart 归属**，不得只说「part 1」。
