# 低酸罐头与酸化食品的法定要求（21 CFR Part 113 / 114 法规正文）（lacf-statutory-requirements）

*来源分层：本卡取用**三套法规正文**，均具法律约束力。① **21 CFR Part 113**〔§ 113.xxx〕（Thermally Processed Low-Acid Foods Packaged in Hermetically Sealed Containers，4-1-25 Edition，36 页）；② **21 CFR Part 114**〔§ 114.xxx〕（Acidified Foods，4-1-25 Edition，7 页）；③ **21 CFR Part 117**〔§ 117.xxx〕（CGMP & PCHF，本包主来源）。三者是**并行法条**，不是上下位、也不是新旧替代关系；接口是〔§ 117.1(c)〕并行条款与〔§ 117.5(d)(1)(2)〕**条件性、有限**豁免。*

*⚠ **本卡改变了全包效力分层表述**：自此「法规正文层」含 Part 117 / 113 / 114 三套独立编号（**v4.9 增为四套：加 Part 123〔§ 123.xxx〕，见 CAP-22 `seafood-haccp-statutory-requirements`**）。`regulatory-boundaries` 里「Part 117 是唯一法定义务判定基准」只能理解为「判定 **Part 117 义务**的唯一基准」，不能解释成「全包唯一法条」。与 CP 7303.070（检查员作业指导，CAP-19）、PCHF 指南、L.mono 指南、SECG、Ed.5、Handbook、CPGM 7303.040、CP 7303.050、IOM 2026 Ch.5 合计 **二十八类不可混引**。*

*⚠ **part 108 自 v4.12 起已收**（第十七来源，CAP-25 `emergency-permit-control`）：FCE 注册 / SID 定时工艺备案 / Emergency Permit Control / §108.35 BPCS 认可现按〔§ 108.xxx〕引用，不再标「转引未收」。**仍未收的是 part 110**（旧 CGMP，含 §110.110，正文未收）——涉及 §110.110 一律标「转引未收」，不得从 Part 113/114 或 CP 反推条文。*

*本卡定位：CAP-19 `lacf-inspection-program` 给的是**检查员会查什么、什么数值触发升级**；本卡给的是**企业法定义务写在哪些条、怎样与 Part 117 衔接**。两张卡必须并用，不得拿其中一张的数字或结论替代另一张。*

## R — 原文

**① 三套法规正文并行，且 §117.5(d) 是「条件性 + 有限」豁免（逐字）**：

> "Food covered by specific current good manufacturing practice regulations also is subject to the requirements of those regulations."
> —— 〔§ 117.1(c)〕

> "Subparts C and G of this part do not apply with respect to activities that are subject to part 113 of this chapter (Thermally Processed Low-Acid Foods Packaged in Hermetically Sealed Containers) at a facility if you are required to comply with, and are in compliance with, part 113 of this chapter with respect to such activities."
> —— 〔§ 117.5(d)(1)〕；注意 `required to comply with, and are in compliance with` 是**两个并列条件**

> "The exemption in paragraph (d)(1) of this section is applicable only with respect to the microbiological hazards that are regulated under part 113 of this chapter."
> —— 〔§ 117.5(d)(2)〕

**② §114.5 把 parts 110 and 117 明文拉进酸化食品掺假判定（逐字，本轮最重要的纠错依据）**：

> "The criteria in §§ 114.10, 114.80, 114.83, 114.89, and 114.100, as well as the criteria in parts 110 and 117 of this chapter, apply in determining whether an article of acidified food is adulterated:"
> —— 〔§ 114.5〕，FR citation `[80 FR 56144, Sept. 17, 2015]`

**③ Part 113 的法定身份边界与企业义务（逐字）**：

> "Low-acid foods means any foods, other than alcoholic beverages, with a finished equilibrium pH greater than 4.6 and a water activity (aw) greater than 0.85. Tomatoes and tomato products having a finished equilibrium pH less than 4.7 are not classed as low-acid foods."
> —— 〔§ 113.3(n)〕

> "The criteria in §§ 113.10, 113.40, 113.60, 113.81, 113.83, 113.87, 113.89, and 113.100 shall apply in determining whether the facilities, methods, practices, and controls used by the commercial processor in the manufacture, processing, or packing of low-acid foods in hermetically sealed containers are operated or administered in a manner adequate to protect the public health."
> —— 〔§ 113.5〕

> "This person shall supervise only in those areas for which a school approved by the Commissioner identifies the person as having satisfactorily completed training."
> —— 〔§ 113.10〕

> "Scheduled processes for low-acid foods shall be established by qualified persons having expert knowledge of thermal processing requirements for low-acid foods in hermetically sealed containers and having adequate facilities for making such determinations."
> —— 〔§ 113.83〕

> "Critical factors, e.g., minimum headspace, consistency, maximum fill-in or drained weight, aw, etc., that may affect the scheduled process, shall be specified in the scheduled process."
> —— 〔§ 113.83〕

> "Scheduled processes must be made readily available to the supervisor and any duly authorized employee of the Food and Drug Administration."
> —— 〔§ 113.87(b)〕；这是**法条直接给 FDA 调阅权**，不是 CP 的检查惯例

> "A system for product traffic control in the retort room shall be established to prevent unretorted product from bypassing the retort process."
> —— 〔§ 113.87(b)〕

> "Whenever any process is less than the scheduled process or when critical factors are out of control for any low-acid food or container system as disclosed from records by processor check or otherwise, the commercial processor of that low-acid food shall either fully reprocess that portion of the production involved"
> —— 〔§ 113.89〕；另一条路径是由 competent processing authority 评价后放行或销毁，两类处置都要单独建档

> "Processing and production information shall be entered at the time it is observed by the retort or processing system operator, or other designated person, on forms that include the product, the code number, the date, the retort or processing system number, the size of container"
> —— 〔§ 113.100(a)〕

> "Records of this part may be maintained electronically, provided they are in compliance with part 11 of this chapter."
> —— 〔§ 113.100(h)〕

**④ Part 114 的法定身份边界与企业义务（逐字）**：

> "Acid foods means foods that have a natural pH of 4.6 or below."
> —— 〔§ 114.3(a)〕

> "They have a water activity (aw) greater than 0.85 and have a finished equilibrium pH of 4.6 or below."
> —— 〔§ 114.3(b)〕`acidified foods` 定义；同一款还排除碳酸饮料、果酱果冻蜜饯、与主导酸无显著差异的酸性食品、**冷藏储运零售食品**

> "Shall is used to state mandatory requirements."
> —— 〔§ 114.3(f)〕

> "Should is used to state recommended or advisory procedures or to identify recommended equipment."
> —— 〔§ 114.3(g)〕；**本 part 自己定义了 shall / should**，读条文须按此区分，不得套 PCHF 指南的 "we recommend" 口径

> "All operators of processing and packaging systems shall be under the operating supervisions of a person who has attended a school approved by the Commissioner for giving instruction in food-handling techniques, food-protection principles, personal hygiene and plant sanitation practices, pH controls and critical factors in acidification"
> —— 〔§ 114.10〕；§113.10 的课程要求针对热加工/无菌系统，§114.10 针对酸化，**两张 BPCS 证书覆盖领域不同**

> "Acidified foods shall be so manufactured, processed, and packaged that a finished equilibrium pH value of 4.6 or lower is achieved within the time designated in the scheduled process and maintained in all finished foods."
> —— 〔§ 114.80(a)(1)〕

> "Acidified foods shall be thermally processed to an extent that is sufficient to destroy the vegetative cells of microorganisms of public health significance"
> —— 〔§ 114.80(a)(1)〕；酸化**不等于免除热处理**

> "If the finished equilibrium pH of the food is above 4.0, the measurement of the finished equilibrium pH shall be by a potentiometric method, and the in-process measurements by titration or colorimetry shall be related to the finished equilibrium pH."
> —— 〔§ 114.80(a)(2)〕；pH ≤ 4.0 才可用任意适宜方法

> "The scheduled process shall be established by a qualified person who has expert knowledge acquired through appropriate training and experience in the acidification and processing of acidified foods."
> —— 〔§ 114.83〕

> "Whenever any process operation deviates from the scheduled process for any acidified food and/or the equilibrium pH of the finished product is higher than 4.6, the commercial processor of the acidified food shall either:"
> —— 〔§ 114.89〕

> "(b) thermally process it as a low-acid food under part 113 of this chapter;"
> —— 〔§ 114.89(b)〕；三条法定处置路径之一，**这是 Part 113 与 Part 114 在条文里的直接接口**

> "Copies of all records provided for in paragraphs (b), (c), and (d) of this section shall be retained at the processing plant or other reasonably accessible location for a period of 3 years from the date of manufacture."
> —— 〔§ 114.100(e)〕

## I — 自述

我管的是「**法条写了什么**」。v4.6 及以前，本包讲 LACF/AF 只能靠 CP 7303.070 的检查员口径反推义务，凡涉及 21 CFR 108/113/114 一律写「转引未收」——那是诚实的，但也是残缺的。现在 Part 113 与 Part 114 正文入库，我可以直接给〔§ 113.xxx〕〔§ 114.xxx〕的 `shall` / `must`，并把三处最容易被误读的接口钉死：

1. **并行，不是替代**。〔§ 117.1(c)〕明文「受专项 CGMP 管辖的食品**同时**受那些法规约束」。所以「做 LACF 就不归 Part 117 管」和「做了 PCHF 就不用管 part 113」都是错的。
2. **豁免是条件性的、且只切一小块**。〔§ 117.5(d)(1)〕要同时满足 `required to comply with` **和** `in compliance with` part 113；〔§ 117.5(d)(2)〕把豁免限定在 part 113 管辖的**微生物危害**。化学、物理、以及未被热处理控制而需要预防控制的危害，仍归 Part 117 Subparts C/G。企业若 part 113 不合规，豁免根本不成立。
3. **酸化食品没有这扇门**。〔§ 117.5〕的清单是 part 123（(b)）、part 120（(c)）、part 113（(d)），**没有 part 114**；〔§ 114.5〕还反过来把 parts 110 and 117 拉进掺假判定。所以 AF 企业是「Part 114 义务 + Part 117 Subparts B/C/G」叠加。

第四件事是纠错：**Part 110 至今有效**。本包 v4.3 曾判「Part 110 已于 2018-09-17 移除、Handbook 的 `110.110` 是过时引用」，这与 eCFR 现行文本和 FDA 2018 年规则不符；本卡在 B2 给出更正。我拒绝三件事：把 CP 7303.070 的检查动作写成企业法定义务；从 Part 113/114 反推 part 108/110 的条文；把 4.40 / 4.65 / 4.75 这类 CP 实验室触发线写成法规放行标准。

## A1 — 书中案例

**案例 1：LACF 厂长说「我们免 PCHF，不做危害分析」。**
先问两个条件：是否 `required to comply with` part 113、**且** `in compliance with` part 113（§ 117.5(d)(1)）。若工艺偏离、scheduled process 未按 §113.83 由 qualified person 确立、或 §113.87 的杀菌室规程未张贴，豁免不成立。即便成立，豁免也只覆盖 part 113 管辖的微生物危害（§ 117.5(d)(2)）：过敏原、化学危害、物理危害、以及 `Staphylococcus aureus` 生长产毒等未被热处理控制且需 PC 的危害，仍须按 Subparts C/G 做危害分析与预防控制。正确输出是「**分危害切分**，不是整厂免检」。

**案例 2：酸化食品厂说「我们按 part 114 做就行了」。**
〔§ 117.5〕没有 part 114 豁免；〔§ 114.5〕还明文把 parts 110 and 117 与 §§114.10/114.80/114.83/114.89/114.100 一起用于判定掺假。所以该企业既要做 §114.80 的成品平衡 pH ≤4.6（在 scheduled process 指定时间内达到并维持）与热处理，也要做 Part 117 Subpart B 的 CGMP、Subparts C/G 的危害分析与预防控制。规模小不能免 part 114 义务 —— 这是 CAP-19 从检查员侧给出的同一结论，现在有法条级依据。

**案例 3：供应商资料写「21 CFR 110.110 已作废，符合缺陷行动水平就没事」。**
两半都要纠正。① `§110.110` **不是作废条款**：2015 最终规则的删除指令已被 FDA 2018-09-12 撤回，Part 110 至今有效（详见 B2）；但本包**未收 part 110 正文**，须标「转引未收」。② 「符合 action level 就没事」在任何体系下都错：〔§ 117.110(a)〕是 `lowest level currently feasible` 持续义务，〔§ 117.110(b)〕禁止掺混稀释，§ 117.3 定义用的是 FDA `may regard ... adulterated` 的执法裁量。手册那句 "Poor manufacturing practices may result in enforcement action without regard to the action level" 是 **§110.110(c)** 的意译，**不得标成〔§ 117.110(c)〕**——Part 117 的 §117.110 只有 (a)(b) 两款，但理由不是「part 110 已删」，而是「那是 part 110 的 (c) 款」。

**案例 4：新品成品平衡 pH 4.5、aw 0.94，问「合不合规」。**
三套数字各管一件事，不能用一套答全部：① **法定身份**：pH 4.5 ≤ 4.6 且 aw > 0.85 → 〔§ 114.3(b)〕的 acidified food，须走 Part 114（且 `Keep Refrigerated` 才排除）；② **检查员触发线**：CP 7303.070 PART IV.2 的 4.40 会触发第二分析师同日复测、4.65 触发各测余下半数，4.5 落在复测区；③ **建议层**：L.mono 2017 草案的抑制型配方基准是 pH ≤4.4 / aw ≤0.92，4.5 不满足其 listeristatic 目标。结论：法规身份成立 ≠ 检查不会升级 ≠ 满足某指南建议，三层分别回答。

**案例 5：一批 AF 成品 pH 测到 4.7，QA 想「降酸复用」。**
〔§ 114.89〕给的是三条法定路径：fully reprocess（由 competent processing authority 确立的工艺）、**按 part 113 作为低酸食品热处理**、或隔离后由 competent processing authority 评价；评价未证明安全则返工或销毁，且须留评价程序与结果记录。不是「再加点酸调到 4.5 就放行」。同时〔§ 114.80(a)(2)〕要求成品 pH > 4.0 时用电位法测定 —— 方法本身也是义务，不是选配。

## E — 执行要点

**Step 1 — 先定产品身份，再选法条。** 用成品平衡 pH 与 aw 判轨道：pH > 4.6 且 aw > 0.85 → 〔§ 113.3(n)〕LACF（番茄及番茄制品 pH < 4.7 例外）；低酸原料加酸、aw > 0.85 且成品平衡 pH ≤ 4.6 → 〔§ 114.3(b)〕酸化食品；天然 pH ≤ 4.6 → 〔§ 114.3(a)〕酸性食品；**冷藏储运零售**、碳酸饮料、果酱果冻蜜饯、与主导酸无显著差异者 → §114.3(b) 排除项（转 CP 7303.050 / 7303.040）。法定身份有争议时由 HFP 判定，本包不替企业下结论。

**Step 3 — 切 PCHF 豁免。** 只有 LACF 且满足 §117.5(d)(1) 双条件时，才能把 **part 113 管辖的微生物危害**从 Subparts C/G 移出；其余危害、全部 AF、以及 part 113 不合规的情形，一律留在 Part 117 体系内。写成矩阵，不要写成「免 / 不免」二值。

## B — 边界

**B1 — 21 CFR part 108 的缺口已于 v4.12 闭合（本卡此前的最大缺口）。** FCE（Food Canning Establishment）注册、SID / scheduled process 备案、Emergency Permit Control、§108.35 的 BPCS 认可都在 part 108；§114.10 明文回指 `§ 108.35 and part 113 of this chapter before March 16, 1979`，CP 7303.070 PART I/II 也反复援引。该件自 v4.12 起已作为**第十七来源**纳入（`source-cfr-part108-2025.md`），条款级手续义务见 **CAP-25 `emergency-permit-control`**〔§ 108.xxx〕——含注册（Form FDA 2541，10 天内／外国加工者报检前）、工艺备案（2541e/2541d/2541f/2541g，60 天内）、许可裁定与中止恢复、无证生产的产品放行闸门。仍不给的是 2541 系列**表单栏目细则**与 FURLS / Center Views 操作。⚠ 本卡与 CAP-25 的分工：本卡＝实体工艺法条层（Part 113/114），CAP-25＝手续与程序法条层（Part 108），**不得互相反推**。`part120.pdf` 正文本版仍未纳入。

**B2 — Part 110 至今有效；v4.3 的「110.110 过时引用」结论须更正（本轮实质性纠错）。** 事实链：2015-09-17 PCHF 最终规则（80 FR 55908）的 **amendatory instruction 13**（56144 页）指示 Federal Register 于 **2018-09-17 remove and reserve part 110**；FDA 在 **2018-09-12 最终规则（FR Doc. 2018-19855，同日生效）撤回该指令**，官方措辞 `amendatory instruction 13 in the third column on page 56144 is withdrawn` 与 `Retaining the Human Food CGMP regulation will maintain the status quo ... and will avoid an unintended gap in public health protection`——因部分小/极小微企业 part 117 合规日期延长至 2020-01-27，若删除会有 4–16 个月 CGMP 空档；FDA 并称将在所有企业达到 part 117 CGMP 合规日后另案移除。**eCFR 现行版 Part 110 仍列 11 个 sections（含 §110.110），§114.5 并列 `parts 110 and 117` 也是现行文本**，不是笔误或过时引用。由此：① Handbook 引言的 `21 CFR 110.110` 指向**仍有效但正文未收**的旧 CGMP 条款；② 手册 "Poor manufacturing practices may result in enforcement action without regard to the action level" 是 **§110.110(c)** 的意译，**仍不得标成〔§ 117.110(c)〕**，但理由是「那是 part 110 的 (c) 款」，不再是「part 110 已删」；③ 〔§ 117.110〕只有 (a)(b) 两款、`lowest level currently feasible` + 掺混稀释禁令的结论**不变**；④ 本包未收 part 110 正文，讨论时一律标「转引未收」。⚠ 2018-19855 正文属**外部核实、本包未收**，引用须注明是 Federal Register 规则而非本包来源；`defect-action-levels` B2 / `cgmp-compliance-guide` B11 与 test-prompts 中的对应用例已按本条同步。

**B3 — 三套数字不可互替。** 4.6 / 0.85（〔§ 113.3(n)〕〔§ 114.3(b)(d)〕法定身份）、4.0（〔§ 114.80(a)(2)〕方法切换）、4.40 / 4.65 / 4.75 / 0.90 / 0.93 / 1% / 24 罐（CP 7303.070 检查员实验室触发线）、≤4.4 / ≤0.92（L.mono 2017 草案建议层）。pH 4.5 的 AF 在法定层成立、会触发 CP 复测、却不满足 L.mono 抑制目标 —— 三层分别回答。

**B4 — 法规正文 ≠ 检查员作业指导。** 本卡的 `shall` / `must` 是企业义务；CP 7303.070 的取样量、双分析师、异常罐判读、Import Alert 建议是 FDA 内部作业口径，**不是企业放行标准**，也不得写成法条。〔§ 113.87(b)〕的 scheduled process 调阅权是法条，与 IOM 的记录索取程序（CAP-18）分属两层。

**B5 — §117.5(d) 不是「LACF 自动豁免 PCHF」。** 双条件 + 仅限微生物危害；且 §117.5(b)(c) 对 part 123 / 120 是同构条件性豁免，**part 114 完全不在清单**。任何「整厂免检」「按 part 113 就不用做危害分析」的说法都要拆回危害层逐条判。

**B6 — 不代写备案、不判法定身份、不出法律结论。** 酸化食品是否属 AF 由 HFP 判定（CP 7303.070 PART III.1.A STEP 3）；FCE/SID 须在 FURLS / Center Views 核验；Emergency Permit、Warning Letter、Import Alert 应诉、Warning Letter 答复策略均超出本包。

**B7 — 版本与转换口径。** `4–1–25 Edition` 是 CFR 年度编纂版次，不是 2025 新规；条文修订出处看末尾 FR citation（§114.5 为 `[80 FR 56144, Sept. 17, 2015]`，§113.100 为 `[44 FR 16215 ... as amended at 76 FR 11923, Mar. 3, 2011]`）。两份 PDF 是 GPO 双栏印刷版连续页截取，已按「页 → 左栏 → 右栏」重排、裁掉首尾相邻 part 残段、保留印刷断词连字符（Part 113 933 处 / Part 114 210 处）；逐字引证按归一化口径（去换行、空格、连字符）核对，**检索以 § 编号为准**；§114.3(b) 的 `''pickled ______.''` 是 PDF 文本层的下划线填空，照录但不得推定省略词。
