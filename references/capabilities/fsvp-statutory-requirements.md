# FSVP 法规正文：外国供应商验证程序（21 CFR Part 1 Subpart L）（fsvp-statutory-requirements）

*本卡定位：给**进口商（FSVPI）的法定义务链**——谁算进口商、哪份 food 要一份 FSVP、同一保护水平的标准是什么、危害分析写到什么程度、供应商怎么批准与再评估、四类验证活动何时必须用现场审核、五种免验证情形要满足什么书面对价、入境时怎么标识、记录存几年存哪里。⚠ **与 CAP-21 是两层不是一件**：CAP-21 取用 **CP 7303.878**（检查员侧作业程序，不创设企业义务），本卡取用**法规正文**（企业义务的唯一判定基准）。检查员会怎么查走 CAP-21；企业必须做到什么走本卡。与 CAP-07 的分流：Part 117 Subpart G 供应链程序约束**接收设施**（FD&C Act §418），FSVP 约束**进口商**（§805）——两套法源、两类主体，互不替代。*

## R — 原文

**① 三个定义决定「谁承担义务、义务落在哪份 food 上」（§1.500）**：

> "Importer means the U.S. owner or consignee of an article of food that is being offered for import into the United States. If there is no U.S. owner or consignee of an article of food at the time of U.S. entry, the importer is the U.S. agent or representative of the foreign owner or consignee at the time of entry, as confirmed in a signed statement of consent to serve as the importer under this subpart."
> —— 〔§ 1.500〕[pdf-page 2]；**进口商是美国主体**，不是境外出口商。无美国货主时才轮到美国代理，且须有**签署的同意书**

> "Foreign supplier means, for an article of food, the establishment that manufactures/processes the food, raises the animal, or grows the food that is exported to the United States without further manufacturing/processing by another establishment, except for further manufacturing/processing that consists solely of the addition of labeling or any similar activity of a de minimis nature."
> —— 〔§ 1.500〕[pdf-page 2]；**只加贴标签不算「进一步加工」**，故该实体仍是 foreign supplier

> "Very small importer means: (1) With respect to the importation of human food, an importer (including any subsidiaries and affiliates) averaging less than $1 million per year, adjusted for inflation, during the 3-year period preceding the applicable calendar year, in sales of human food combined with the U.S. market value of human food imported, manufactured, processed, packed, or held without sale"
> —— 〔§ 1.500〕[pdf-page 3–4]；人类食品 **<100 万美元/年**，动物食品另有 **<250 万美元/年** 一款；两者都**含子公司与关联企业**，且把「未销售部分的美国市场价值」计入

**② 适用范围与七项豁免（§1.501）**：

> "(b) Exemptions for juice and seafood — (1) ... This subpart does not apply with respect to juice, fish, and fishery products that are imported from a foreign supplier that is required to comply with, and is in compliance with, the requirements in part 120 or part 123 of this chapter. If you import juice or fish and fishery products that are subject to part 120 or part 123, respectively, you must comply with the requirements applicable to importers of those products under § 120.14 or § 123.12 of this chapter, respectively."
> —— 〔§ 1.501(b)(1)〕[pdf-page 5]；**豁免是对价式的**：不是「不用管」，而是义务**换轨**到〔§ 120.14〕或〔§ 123.12〕的进口商要求。且前提是境外供应商「须遵守且正在遵守」part 120/123

> "(f) Inapplicability to food that is transshipped or imported for processing and export. This subpart does not apply to food: (1) That is transshipped through the United States to another country and is not sold or distributed to the public in the United States; or (2) That is imported for processing and future export and that is not sold or distributed to the public in the United States."
> —— 〔§ 1.501(f)〕[pdf-page 6]；**「不进入美国市场」是共同条件**，转运与加工后出口两种情形都以此划线

**③ FSVP 义务本体：同一保护水平标准（§1.502(a)）**：

> "...for each food you import, you must develop, maintain, and follow an FSVP that provides adequate assurances that your foreign supplier is producing the food in compliance with processes and procedures that provide at least the same level of public health protection as those required under section 418 (regarding hazard analysis and risk-based preventive controls for certain foods) or 419 (regarding standards for produce safety), if either is applicable, and the implementing regulations, and is producing the food in compliance with sections 402 (regarding adulteration) and 403(w) (if applicable)..."
> —— 〔§ 1.502(a)〕[pdf-page 6]；判定标尺是**「至少同等水平的公共卫生保护」**，不是「符合出口国法律」，也不是「有第三方证书」

**④ deemed compliance 有一处硬例外（§1.502(c)）**：

> "You are deemed to be in compliance with the requirements of this subpart for a food you import, except for the requirements in § 1.509, if you are a receiving facility as defined in § 117.3 or § 507.3 of this chapter and you are in compliance with the following requirements of part 117 or part 507 of this chapter, as applicable"
> —— 〔§ 1.502(c)〕[pdf-page 6]；**`except for the requirements in § 1.509`**——接收设施即便 deemed compliance，**仍须在每一 entry line 申报 UFI/DUNS**。这是全 subpart 最容易被漏的一句

**⑤ 危害分析必须书面，且不因结论为「无」而免除（§1.504）**：

> "you must conduct a hazard analysis to identify and evaluate, based on experience, illness data, scientific reports, and other information, known or reasonably foreseeable hazards for each type of food you import to determine whether there are any hazards requiring a control. Your hazard analysis must be written regardless of its outcome."
> —— 〔§ 1.504(a)〕[pdf-page 7]；`written regardless of its outcome` 与 CAP-01 的「No 也要举证」同源

> "your hazard analysis must include an evaluation of the hazards identified in paragraph (b) of this section to assess the probability that the hazard will occur in the absence of controls and the severity of the illness or injury if the hazard were to occur."
> —— 〔§ 1.504(c)(1)〕[pdf-page 8]；概率按**「不存在控制时」**评估——与§117.130(c)(1)(i) 的反事实基准同一口径

> "you must determine whether there are any other types of hazards requiring a control in such food."
> —— 〔§ 1.504(e)〕[pdf-page 8]（前句为：`you are not required to determine whether there are any biological hazards requiring a control in such food`）；**covered produce 的生物危害可免判，其余危害仍须判**

**⑥ 严重危害触发「初次进口前 + 至少每年」现场审核（§1.506(d)(2)）**：

> "When a hazard in a food will be controlled by the foreign supplier and is one for which there is a reasonable probability that exposure to the hazard will result in serious adverse health consequences or death to humans or animals, you must conduct or obtain documentation of an onsite audit of the foreign supplier before initially importing the food and at least annually thereafter, unless you make an adequate written determination that, instead of such initial and annual onsite auditing, other supplier verification activities ... and/or less frequent onsite auditing are appropriate"
> —— 〔§ 1.506(d)(2)〕[pdf-page 11]；两项要件同时成立才触发：**危害由境外供应商控制** + **暴露后可能造成严重健康后果或死亡**。要绕开必须做出**书面判定**

> "You may not rely on the foreign supplier itself or employees of the foreign supplier to perform supplier verification activities, except with respect to sampling and testing of food"
> —— 〔§ 1.506(e)(2)(ii)〕[pdf-page 13]；**供应商自证无效**，唯一例外是抽样检测。要求供应商「自己出一份审核报告」不构成验证活动

> "If the onsite audit is conducted solely to meet the requirements of paragraph (e) of this section by an audit agent of a certification body that is accredited in accordance with subpart M of this part, the audit is not subject to the requirements in that subpart."
> —— 〔§ 1.506(e)(1)(i)(C)〕[pdf-page 12]；**为满足本 subpart 而做的认证机构审核，反过来不受 subpart M 的审核员监管约束**

**⑦ 五种免评估/免验证情形与书面保证三要素（§1.507）**：

> "You are not required to conduct an evaluation of a food and foreign supplier under § 1.505 or supplier verification activities under § 1.506 when you identify a hazard requiring a control (identified hazard) in a food and any of the following circumstances apply"
> —— 〔§ 1.507(a)〕[pdf-page 14]；五种情形 (a)(1)–(5)：① 该食品**不经控制不能食用**（如可可豆、咖啡豆）；② 依赖受 Part 117/507 Subpart C 约束的**客户**控制（须披露 + 年度书面保证）；③ 依赖**不受** Subpart C 约束的客户（须披露 + 年度书面保证）；④ 依赖客户的下游实体控制（须披露 + 年度保证 + 传递条款）；⑤ 自有**后续分销环节控制系统**并记录其实施

> "Any written assurances required under this section must contain the following: (1) Effective date; (2) Printed names and signatures of authorized officials; and (3) The assurance specified in the applicable paragraph."
> —— 〔§ 1.507(b)〕[pdf-page 14]；**三要素缺一即不成立**。与 CAP-07 的书面保证要件同源，但此处的条款出处是〔§ 1.507(b)〕而非〔§ 117.335〕

> "The customer or other subsequent entity in the distribution chain for a food that provides a written assurance under paragraph (a)(2), (3), or (4) of this section must act consistently with the assurance and document its actions taken to satisfy the written assurance."
> —— 〔§ 1.507(c)〕[pdf-page 14]；**保证书不是纸面免责**，出具方须与其保证一致行动并留记录

**⑧ 入境标识：每一 entry line（§1.509）**：

> "You must ensure that, for each line entry of food product offered for importation into the United States, your name, electronic mail address, and unique facility identifier recognized as acceptable by FDA, identifying you as the importer of the food, are provided electronically when filing entry with U.S. Customs and Border Protection."
> —— 〔§ 1.509(a)〕[pdf-page 16]；**`each line entry`**——不是每票、不是每年，是**每一条报关行**

**⑨ 记录：签日期、2 年、24 小时、part 11 豁免、英文翻译（§1.510）**：

> "You must sign and date records concerning your FSVP upon initial completion and upon any modification of the FSVP."
> —— 〔§ 1.510(a)(2)〕[pdf-page 16]

> "Offsite storage of records, including records maintained by other entities in accordance with § 1.504, § 1.505, or § 1.506, is permitted if such records can be retrieved and provided onsite within 24 hours of request for official review. Electronic records are considered to be onsite if they are accessible from an onsite location."
> —— 〔§ 1.510(b)(2)〕[pdf-page 16]；与 Part 121 不同，**FSVP 没有「必须留现场」的文件**，全部可异地 + 24 小时

> "you must retain records referenced in this subpart until at least 2 years after you created or obtained the records."
> —— 〔§ 1.510(c)(1)〕[pdf-page 16]；另有（c)(2) **程序类记录在停用后再存 2 年**

> "Records that are established or maintained to satisfy the requirements of this subpart and that meet the definition of electronic records in § 11.3(b)(6) of this chapter are exempt from the requirements of part 11 of this chapter. Records that satisfy the requirements of this subpart, but that also are required under other applicable statutory provisions or regulations, remain subject to part 11."
> —— 〔§ 1.510(d)〕[pdf-page 16]；与 CAP-32 的〔§ 121.305(g)〕同构：**豁免只覆盖为本 subpart 而建的记录**

**⑩ 极小进口商与「等效国家」两套简化路径（§1.512 / §1.513）**：

> "If you are a very small importer, for each food you import, you must obtain written assurance, before importing the food and at least every 2 years thereafter, that your foreign supplier is producing the food in compliance with processes and procedures that provide at least the same level of public health protection as those required under section 418 or 419"
> —— 〔§ 1.512(b)(3)(i)〕[pdf-page 21]；**每 2 年**一次的书面保证，即可替代〔§ 1.504〕–〔§ 1.508〕与〔§ 1.510〕[pdf-page 21 的 (b)(2)]

> "you must document that the foreign supplier is in, and under the regulatory oversight of, a country whose food safety system FDA has officially recognized as comparable or determined to be equivalent to that of the United States, and that the food is within the scope of that official recognition or equivalency determination."
> —— 〔§ 1.513(b)(1)〕[pdf-page 24]；等效国家路径免〔§ 1.504〕–〔§ 1.508〕，仍须〔§ 1.503〕〔§ 1.509〕〔§ 1.510〕，且**只适用于不再进一步加工的食品**

**⑪ 后果：两条执法钩子（§1.514）**：

> "An article of food is subject to refusal of admission under section 801(a)(3) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act if it appears that the importer of that food fails to comply with this subpart with respect to that food."
> —— 〔§ 1.514(a)〕[pdf-page 25]

> "The importation or offering for importation into the United States of an article of food without the importer having an FSVP that meets the requirements of section 805 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, including the requirements of this subpart, is prohibited under section 301(zz) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act."
> —— 〔§ 1.514(b)〕[pdf-page 25]；**拒绝入境（§801(a)(3)）**与**禁止行为（§301(zz)）**是两个独立后果，不可只提一个

## I — 自述

**一份 FSVP 对应一份 food，不是一家供应商。**〔§ 1.502(a)〕的落点是 `for each food you import`。同一境外供应商供三种食品，就是三份 FSVP；反过来，同一食品换供应商须走〔§ 1.505〕的重新评估。

**义务链的六个环节**（本卡的主干）：

| 环节 | 条款 | 一句话 |
|---|---|---|
| 判主体 | 〔§ 1.500〕 | 进口商＝美国货主/收货人；无则美国代理 + 签署同意书 |
| 判适用 | 〔§ 1.501〕 | 七项豁免，其中 juice/seafood 是**换轨**（§120.14 / §123.12）而非免除 |
| 判标准 | 〔§ 1.502〕 | 「至少同等公共卫生保护水平」= §418/§419 + §402/§403(w) |
| 做评估 | 〔§ 1.504–1.505〕 | 书面危害分析（无论结论）→ 四因素评估 → 批准并记录 |
| 做验证 | 〔§ 1.506–1.507〕 | 四类活动；严重危害须初次前 + 每年现场审核；五种情形可免 |
| 留证据 | 〔§ 1.509–1.510〕 | 每 entry line 报 UFI；记录 2 年、异地 24 小时 |

**与三张卡的接口（容易混的地方）**：

1. **CAP-21 `fsvp-inspection-program`（CP 7303.878）**——同一 subject 的**检查员侧**。CAP-21 给「检查员会索要哪六组记录、483a 怎么分级、Import Alert 99-41 怎么列名」；本卡给「这些记录本来该由哪条法规要求、要求到什么程度」。**不得从 CP 反推条文，也不得用本卡替代 CP 的检查动作**。
2. **CAP-07 `supply-chain-verification`（Part 117 Subpart G）**——约束**接收设施**。一个既进口又接收的工厂可能两套都要：对外购原料走 Subpart G，对进口食品走 FSVP；〔§ 1.502(c)〕的 deemed compliance 正是两者的接缝，且留了〔§ 1.509〕这个例外。
3. **CAP-22 / CAP-26（Part 123 / Part 120 进口商义务）**——〔§ 1.501(b)〕把 juice/seafood 换轨到〔§ 120.14〕/〔§ 123.12〕。**别把 FSVP 的验证活动套到这两类产品上**，它们的进口商义务在各自 part 里。

**规模**：25 页 / 15 条款（§1.500–1.514）/ 无表格、无 IBR 外部文件。

## A1 — 判定场景（由条文推出，非执法案例）

| 场景 | 判定路径 | 结论 |
|---|---|---|
| 中国出口商直接卖给美国超市，美国超市是收货人 | 〔§ 1.500〕importer = U.S. owner or consignee | 义务在**美国超市**，不在中国工厂；但工厂会被要求配合提供记录 |
| 无美国货主，货到美国才找代理清关 | 〔§ 1.500〕+〔§ 1.509(b)〕 | 境外货主须**在进口前**指定美国代理，且有签署同意书；否则不得进口〔§ 1.514(a)〕 |
| 进口果汁，境外工厂已按 Part 120 做 HACCP | 〔§ 1.501(b)(1)〕换轨 | 不适用 FSVP，但**须符合〔§ 120.14〕进口商义务**——不是「什么都不用做」 |
| 进口原料用于自己加工成罐装低酸食品 | 〔§ 1.502(b)(2)〕 | 微生物危害部分按 Part 113 合规即免；**其他危害仍须 FSVP** |
| 自己是接收设施，已按 Part 117 建立供应链程序 | 〔§ 1.502(c)〕deemed compliance | 视为合规，**但〔§ 1.509〕的每 entry line UFI 仍要做** |
| 进口可可豆，自己在美加工时才烘焙 | 〔§ 1.507(a)(1)〕 | 可免〔§ 1.505〕/〔§ 1.506〕，须**判定并记录**「不经控制不能食用」 |
| 进口生坚果，客户是受 Subpart C 约束的加工厂 | 〔§ 1.507(a)(2)〕 | 须随货披露 `not processed to control [identified hazard]` + **每年**取得客户书面保证（三要素齐全） |
| 进口膳食补充剂成品 | 〔§ 1.511(c)〕 | 免〔§ 1.504〕〔§ 1.506〕〔§ 1.507〕，但〔§ 1.503〕〔§ 1.505 部分〕〔§ 1.508–1.510〕仍须做 |
| 年进口额 80 万美元的人类食品进口商 | 〔§ 1.500〕very small importer（<100 万，3 年均值，含关联企业） | 可走〔§ 1.512〕：书面保证每 2 年一次，免〔§ 1.504〕–〔§ 1.508〕 |

## A2 — 未来触发

- **新增一个 food 或新增一个境外供应商** → 新起一份〔§ 1.502(a)〕FSVP + 〔§ 1.504〕危害分析 + 〔§ 1.505〕评估
- **获知供应商被 FDA 警告信/进口警报/其他执法行动** → 〔§ 1.505(a)(1)(iii)(B)〕列入评估因素；已批准的须〔§ 1.505(c)(1)〕**迅速重新评估**并记录
- **三年未重新评估** → 〔§ 1.505(c)(2)〕强制补做；〔§ 1.512(c)(1)(ii)(B)〕对小境外供应商同样设 3 年兜底
- **验证结果不足以证明危害被控制** → 〔§ 1.506(e)(3)〕须按〔§ 1.508(a)〕采取措施（可含停用该供应商）
- **通过验证以外渠道发现问题** → 〔§ 1.508(b)〕须**调查 FSVP 本身是否充分**并酌情修改（这一条常被漏，它不是「处理完这批货」而是「审自己的体系」）
- **销售额跨过 100 万/250 万门槛** → 极小进口商身份失效或恢复，须在当年 12 月 31 日前重新记录〔§ 1.512(b)(1)(i)(A)〕
- **FDA 书面要求电子提交记录** → 〔§ 1.510(b)(3)〕须电子发送，不能只「放厂里等你来看」
- **原产国被 FDA 正式认可/等效** → 可切〔§ 1.513〕路径，但须年度记录状态并监控「良好合规状态」

## 硬数字速查

> 注：全部为 Subpart L **法规正文层**判据。本 subpart 有多组「2 年」，对象各不相同，不可合并。

| 数字 | 含义 | 出处 | 陷阱 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **$1,000,000／年** | 极小进口商（人类食品）年均门槛，3 年均值、含关联企业 | 〔§ 1.500〕very small importer | 要把**未销售部分的美国市场价值**加进去 |
| **$2,500,000／年** | 极小进口商（动物食品）年均门槛 | 同上 | 人/动物食品**两套金额**不可混用 |

## E — 执行要点

**Step 1 — 先判「我是不是 importer」。** 〔§ 1.500〕的 importer 是**美国货主或收货人**；无美国货主时才是美国代理，且须有签署同意书。出口企业常误以为「我是卖家所以我承担 FSVP」——错，义务在美国侧主体；但美国客户会把记录要求传导回来，这是商业压力不是法定义务。

**Step 2 — 逐项过〔§ 1.501〕七项豁免，注意两处「换轨」与一处「同意条件」。** (b) juice/seafood 是**换轨**到〔§ 120.14〕/〔§ 123.12〕，不是免除；(e) 酒精三款须双条件；(f)(g)(h) 的共同条件是**不进入美国市场**或**已受 USDA 管辖**。回答「我们能不能免」必须落到具体款号，不能只说「属于豁免」。

**Step 3 — 先把「同一保护水平」翻译成可核对的对象。** 〔§ 1.502(a)〕的标尺是 §418/§419 + §402/§403(w)。实务含义：**危害分析要对得上 Part 117 的口径、过敏原要对得上 §403(w)**。第三方证书、出口国官方证明都不是这条标准的替代物。

**Step 4 — 危害分析按〔§ 1.504〕写，重点是三个「不能省」。** ① `written regardless of its outcome`（结论为「无」也要写）；② 概率按**不存在控制时**评估〔§ 1.504(c)(1)〕；③ RTE 且包装前暴露、包装后无致死处理时，**必须评估环境病原**〔§ 1.504(c)(2)〕。可以借用他方（含供应商）的危害分析，但须记录**由 qualified individual 完成**〔§ 1.504(d)〕。

**Step 5 — 供应商评估与批准按〔§ 1.505〕做四因素并留痕。** ① 危害分析结果与危害性质；② **由谁控制危害**（供应商本身 / 其原料商 / 供应链他方）；③ 供应商表现，含**是否被 FDA 警告信、进口警报或其他执法行动**；④ 其他因素。评估、批准、再评估**三者都要文档化**；3 年未再评估即违法〔§ 1.505(c)(2)〕。

**Step 6 — 验证活动先判「是否严重危害」，再定活动与频次。** 若危害由境外供应商控制且暴露可能造成严重健康后果或死亡 → **初次进口前 + 至少每年现场审核**〔§ 1.506(d)(2)〕，要绕开须**书面判定**。其余情形按〔§ 1.506(d)(1)〕在四类活动中选择并记录理由。三条硬约束：审核须由 **qualified auditor**〔§ 1.503(b)〕；**不得依赖供应商自己**（抽样检测除外）〔§ 1.506(e)(2)(ii)〕；**财务利益冲突禁止、报酬不得与结果挂钩**〔§ 1.506(e)(4)〕。

**Step 7 — 若要免验证，把〔§ 1.507〕的「对价」凑齐。** 五种情形都要求：随货披露 `not processed to control [identified hazard]`（(a)(1) 除外）+ 年度书面保证（三要素：生效日期、授权官员印刷姓名与签名、保证内容）+ 保证方须**一致行动并记录**〔§ 1.507(c)〕。少一项即回到完整 FSVP 义务。

**Step 8 — 入境标识单独当成一件事做。** 〔§ 1.509〕是**每一 entry line** 必报：名称、邮箱、UFI（FDA 认可的 DUNS 等）。即使按〔§ 1.502(c)〕deemed compliance，这一条仍要做——这是全 subpart 唯一的「不可豁免项」。

**Step 9 — 记录按四件事收口。** ① 签日期：初次完成与每次修改〔§ 1.510(a)(2)〕；② 期限：通用 2 年、程序类停用后再 2 年、极小进口商证明 3 年；③ 可及性：随时提供 + 英文翻译 + 异地 24 小时 + FDA 书面要求时电子发送〔§ 1.510(b)〕；④ part 11：豁免只覆盖为本 subpart 而建的记录〔§ 1.510(d)〕。

**Step 10 — 收尾声明并把检查侧分开。** 每条结论标〔§ 1.5xx〕[pdf-page N]；「检查员会怎么查、483a 怎么分级、Import Alert 99-41 怎么列名」一律转 **CAP-21**，不写进本卡的义务表述。**全包现为二十八类不可混引，共 36 张能力卡（另 20 张子卡）。**

## B — 边界

**B1 — 本卡与 CAP-21 是「企业义务层」与「检查员作业层」的分工，不是同一件事的详略版。** CAP-21 取用 **CP 7303.878**（`Transmittal 2023-CPM-CFSAN-003`），是 FDA 内部作业指导，**不创设企业义务**；本卡取用 §1.500–1.514 正文，是企业义务的**唯一判定基准**。输出时：问「我必须做到什么」→ 本卡；问「FDA 会怎么查、483a 怎么签」→ CAP-21。**不得从 CP 的检查动作反推条文，也不得用本卡的条文去预测检查结论。**

**B2 — 与 Part 117 Subpart G 是两套法源、两类主体。** FSVP 约束**进口商**（FD&C Act §805），供应链程序约束**接收设施**（§418）。同一企业可能两套都适用：进口的食品走 FSVP，国内采购的原料走 Subpart G。**〔§ 1.502(c)〕只是接缝，不是替代**，且它明确留了〔§ 1.509〕这个例外。

**B3 — juice/seafood 的豁免是「换轨」，不是「免除」。** 〔§ 1.501(b)(1)〕把义务接到〔§ 120.14〕或〔§ 123.12〕；〔§ 1.501(b)(2)〕对用作原料的情形另有条件。回答「进口果汁/水产要不要做 FSVP」时，正确表述是「不适用 FSVP，但须履行〔§ 120.14〕/〔§ 123.12〕的进口商义务」，并转 CAP-26 / CAP-22。说成「不用做任何事」是实质错误。

**B4 — deemed compliance 不等于「全部免掉」。** 〔§ 1.502(c)〕三个条件（接收设施 + 分别符合〔§ 117.135〕/〔§ 117.136〕/Subpart G 之一）满足后「视为合规」，但**排除〔§ 1.509〕**。凡输出「我们是接收设施所以不用报 UFI」，即为错引。

**B5 — 「无 hazards requiring a control」的免做范围有两处限制。** 〔§ 1.504(f)〕免的是〔§ 1.505〕评估与〔§ 1.506〕验证，**不免〔§ 1.504〕本身的书面义务**；且该款**不适用于 covered produce**（水果蔬菜类生鲜农产品）。另〔§ 1.504(e)〕只免「生物危害是否需控制的判定」，其余危害仍须判。

**B6 — 供应商自证不构成验证。** 〔§ 1.506(e)(2)(ii)〕唯一例外是抽样检测。因此「供应商发来的自检报告」「供应商自己的 HACCP 证书」都不能单独充当现场审核/记录审查；证书可以是〔§ 1.505(a)(1)(iii)〕评估的一个因素，不是〔§ 1.506〕的验证活动。

**B7 — 现场审核的替代路径有严格前提。** ① 官方检查结果替代须在「本应审核之日」起 **1 年内**〔§ 1.506(e)(1)(i)(E)〕；② 认证机构审核若**仅为满足本 subpart**，则该次审核**不受 subpart M 审核员监管要求约束**〔§ 1.506(e)(1)(i)(C)〕——这一句常被反过来读成「认证审核自动合规」，方向恰好相反。

**B8 — 五组「2 年/3 年」不可互换。** 记录 2 年、程序类停用后 2 年、极小进口商证明 3 年、供应商重评估兜底 3 年、极小进口商书面保证「每 2 年」。把这五个数字压成一个「保存 2 年」是最常见的输出错误。

**B9 — 本卡不处理检查程序、执法裁量与商业实务。** Import Alert 99-41 全文、FSVP Guidance for Industry、IP guidance tabs、HFP Enforcement Bulletin、CP 7303.878 的 PART 结构与 483a 分级 → 均属 CAP-21 或本包未收。另：FSVP 与 CBP 的 entry 系统操作、DUNS 申请、代理协议模板、SRA（Systems Recognition Arrangement）国别清单内容，本卡只给法条要求，不给操作指引。

**B10 — 版本与引证口径。** `up to date as of 11/20/2026` 是 eCFR 快照日期，**不是修订生效日**；Subpart L 的 SOURCE 是 `80 FR 74340, Nov. 27, 2015, as amended at 81 FR 25327, Apr. 28, 2016`。§1.500 的金额门槛是**通胀调整**项，引用具体金额须回 eCFR 当前版核对当年调整值；本卡给的 $1M/$2.5M 是条文基数，不等于当年生效数。

**B11 — §1.227 不属本 subpart。** `farm` 定义外转到〔§ 1.227〕，位于 Part 1 其他位置。引用时写〔§ 1.227〕并注明「本 subpart 之外的定义锚点」，不得写成「§ 1.5xx 系列的 §1.227」。

**B12 — 引用规范（全包统一，二十八类不可混引）。** 〔§ 1.5xx〕独立编号，与〔§ 101 / 108 / 113 / 114 / 117 / 120 / 121 / 123 / 172.xxx〕九套并列；CP 7303.878 用「CP 7303.878 PART x」；IOM 用章号；FDA 指南为 should 层。**尤其不得把〔§ 1.506〕的验证活动与〔§ 117.165〕的验证活动互引**——两者对象（境外供应商 vs 本厂预防控制）与主体（进口商 vs 设施）都不同。
