- id: p4-01
  title: 全员必须成为 qualified individual（含临时与季节工）
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Ch11 §11.10（21 CFR 117.4(b)）
  source_quote: |
    "each individual engaged in manufacturing, processing, packing, or holding food (including
    temporary and seasonal personnel) or in the supervision thereof must: Be a qualified
    individual as that term is defined in 21 CFR 117.3—i.e., have the education, training, or
    experience (or a combination thereof) necessary to manufacture, process, pack, or hold
    clean and safe food as appropriate to the individual's assigned duties"
  summary: |
    进入食品生产、包装、储存环节的每一个人——不排除临时工和季节工——都必须
    具备与其岗位职责相适应的教育、培训或经验组合，才能算"合格人员"。
    监督人员另有加码：负责保证他人合规的主管必须具备监督清洁安全食品生产的资历。
    这条规则把"资格"变成人人有份的底线要求，而不是只对 PCQI 一人。
  tags: [principle, rule, training, qualified-individual, 117.4]

- id: p4-02
  title: 培训记录必须建立并保存
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Ch11 §11.10（21 CFR 117.4(d)）
  source_quote: |
    "Records that document required training must be established and maintained. (See 21 CFR
    117.4(d).)"
  summary: |
    117.4 要求的培训不是"做过就行"——凡法定要求的培训都必须落成记录并持续保存。
    与之配套：所有人员（管理、研发、市场、采购、法务、审核、产品开发、设计工程、
    生产、QA、客诉、仓储配送）都应接受通用过敏原意识培训。
    没有记录 = 无法向检查官证明培训发生过 = 视同未培训。
  tags: [principle, records, training, allergen-awareness]

- id: p4-03
  title: 过敏原意识培训覆盖全员全链条
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Ch11 §11.10
  source_quote: |
    "The successful control of food allergens depends on the involvement of all personnel in
    all phases of product development, ingredient procurement/sourcing, receiving, production
    and distribution of foods... we recommend that all individuals (including individuals with
    responsibility for management, research and development, marketing, procurement,
    legal/regulatory issues, auditing, product development, design engineering, production,
    quality assurance, consumer complaints, and warehousing and distribution) receive general
    allergen awareness training."
  summary: |
    过敏原控制的成败取决于全链条所有人的参与，而不是只靠车间一线。
    FDA 明确建议把管理、研发、市场、采购、法务、审核、工程、QA、客诉、仓储物流
    全部纳入通用过敏原意识培训。培训内容应包含：含过敏原原料识别、厂房设备设计
    如何防交叉接触、本厂各环节防交叉接触程序、人员 CGMP 措施、设备/工具/器具
    移动规则、以及"怀疑出现非预期过敏原时该做什么"。
  tags: [principle, training, allergen, all-personnel]

- id: p4-04
  title: 按岗位职责分配差异化培训主题
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Ch11 §11.10（Table 11-5）
  source_quote: |
    "| Purchasing | Awareness that food allergen hazards may require supply-chain controls with
    associated supplier approval and verification activities | Receiving | Procedures for
    receipt, handling, and storage of allergen-containing raw materials... | Production
    operations | The food allergen control measures employed during production... |
    Maintenance | Measures to prevent allergen cross-contact (e.g., through maintenance tools)
    between production lines |"
  summary: |
    通用培训之外，每个职能都要有针对性主题：采购岗要知道过敏原危害可能触发供应链

    维修工具把过敏原带过生产线；清洁岗要认识清洁程序在防交叉接触中的关键角色；
    主管岗要掌握与本岗位监管责任对应的过敏原程序。
  tags: [principle, checklist, training-matrix, role-based]

- id: p4-05
  title: 做好标签控制比给控制归类更重要
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Ch11 §11.11
  source_quote: |
    "Label controls have a role in ensuring that the finished food will not be misbranded under
    section 403(w) of the FD&C Act. Putting a label control in place to prevent such
    misbranding is more important than classifying that control in a particular way (e.g., as a
    label content control or a label management control)."
  summary: |
    标签控制分"内容控制"（标签写什么）与"管理控制"（对的标签用对的产品）两类，
    但 FDA 明确说：真正防止 403(w) 假标比归类更重要。配套弹性：同一活动可以
    同时作为两类标签控制的监控/验证，同一份记录也可以同时记录两类控制。
    企业不应把精力浪费在分类学上，而应保证标签全生命周期不出错。
  tags: [principle, label-controls, flexibility, misbranding]

- id: p4-06
  title: 排产序列：无过敏原先做、过敏原最多最后做
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Ch11 §11.12.2.1（Dessert Manufacturer A）；附录 11-1.F.2
  source_quote: |
    "produce the foods in a sequence whereby the food with no allergen or the fewest allergens
    is produced first and the food with the most allergens is produced last. As another
    example, you could cluster runs of products containing the same food allergen profile to
    reduce the number of changeovers."
  summary: |
    时间分隔排产是防交叉接触的核心 CGMP 手段：按过敏原含量从少到多安排生产顺序，
    让残留只能"往下走"不会污染前面产品；或把同过敏原谱的批次聚类以减少换产次数。
    Dessert A 示例即"树莓雪葩→香草冰淇淋→香草冻糕→杏仁冻糕"的递增序列。
  tags: [principle, production-scheduling, cross-contact, cgmp]
  repeated_in_chunk: yes（§11.12.2.1 与附录 11-1.F.2 两处）

- id: p4-07
  title: 过敏原成分尽量晚加、尽量靠近下游
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 附录 11-1.F.2
  source_quote: |
    "Add allergenic ingredients as late in the production process as possible, and as far
    downstream as possible in the processing line (e.g., closest to the filling and packaging
    equipment), to minimize the amount of equipment in the production area that comes in
    contact with the allergen."
  summary: |
    把含过敏原配料放在工艺最末端、离灌装包装最近的位置添加，使接触该过敏原的
    设备数量最少。Dessert A 把杏仁放在最后灌装步骤加入正是这一原则的落地。
    与排产原则配合构成"过敏原暴露最小化"的组合拳。
  tags: [principle, process-design, allergen, minimization]

- id: p4-08
  title: 序列内通用清洁够用；回到无过敏原产品前必须专项过敏原清洁
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Ch11 §11.12.3.1（Dessert Manufacturer A）
  source_quote: |
    "The production sequence, coupled with general cleaning measures established in accordance
    with the CGMP requirements of 21 CFR 117.35, is adequate to prevent allergen cross-contact
    after the processing of each of the first three products... General cleaning measures...
    alone are not adequate to prevent allergen cross-contact after the processing of the final
    product... because the production sequence begins again with raspberry sorbet, which
    contains no allergenic ingredients."
  summary: |
    PCQI 的判定规则：若下一个产品的过敏原谱"包含"前一个产品（like into like 方向），
    通用 CGMP 清洁即可；一旦要生产不含前面过敏原的产品（序列回到起点），
    必须执行针对清除所有过敏原残留的专项清洁程序作为预防控制。
    若常规序列被打破（前一产品含后续产品没有的过敏原），同样必须专项清洁。
  tags: [principle, cleaning, allergen, changeover, pcqi-determination]

- id: p4-09
  title: 干清洁禁用压缩空气与喷砂——用真空
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 附录 11-1.D
  source_quote: |
    "Using vacuums, rather than compressed air or grit blasting, for removing dry food residue
    from difficult-to-clean areas, because compressed air and grit blasting can disperse food
    allergen residues from one area to another. Alternatively, if vacuums cannot remove such
    residues, you could disassemble the equipment... If you need to use compressed air or grit
    blasting... you should take precautions to contain food residues that are forcefully
    removed"
  summary: |
    压缩空气和喷砂会把过敏原残留从一个区域吹散到另一个区域，因此难清洁部位的
    干残留应首选真空吸除；真空搞不定就拆机手洗；实在必须用压缩空气/喷砂时，
    必须采取措施围堵被强力吹出的残渣。配套规则：湿区清洁用低压水枪而非高压，
    因为高压会雾化并扩散过敏原残留。
  tags: [principle, prohibition, cleaning, compressed-air, grit-blasting, allergen]
  repeated_in_chunk: yes（附录 11-1.D 一处集中，湿区低压水为同段配套规则）

- id: p4-10
  title: rework "exact into exact" 原则
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 附录 11-1.F.2
  source_quote: |
    "Implement a rework policy for rework to be added back to same finished product (i.e.,
    'exact into exact') whenever practical. Alternatively, add rework to another product with
    the same food allergen profile. Use allergen-containing WIP and rework as soon as possible
    to minimize the potential for the rework to be incorporated into the wrong product."
  summary: |
    返工品回用首选"一模一样进一模一样"（exact into exact）——退回同一成品；
    做不到时只能加入过敏原谱完全相同的其他产品。含过敏原的 WIP/返工品要尽快
    使用以降低进错产品的概率，且必须清晰标识（品名、所含全部过敏原、生产日期、
    将投入哪条线、预计使用日期）并存放在指定加盖区域。
  tags: [principle, rework, exact-into-exact, allergen, wip]

- id: p4-11
  title: 破损容器处置三选一，未围堵不得进生产区
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 附录 11-1.F.1
  source_quote: |
    "If containers have leaks, tears, or other defects, inspect nearby containers for evidence
    of allergen cross-contact. Either reject (or properly dispose of) raw materials... or
    handle damaged containers in a manner that prevents allergen cross-contact. For example,
    you could store damaged containers in a separate location, place a damaged container inside
    another container... You should not move damaged containers into a production area unless
    leakage... has been contained."
  summary: |
    收货时发现容器泄漏、破损：①检查邻近容器是否已被交叉接触；②退货/废弃，
    或③在防交叉接触前提下处置（单独存放、外套一层容器、换装到新容器并明确标识）。
    硬规则：泄漏未围堵之前，破损容器绝不能移入生产区。
  tags: [principle, rule, receiving, damaged-containers, allergen]

- id: p4-12
  title: 无专用库房时，含过敏原原料必须放在不含过敏原原料的下层
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 附录 11-1.F.1
  source_quote: |
    "Segregate allergen-containing raw materials and other ingredients from raw materials and
    other ingredients that do not contain allergens – e.g., in a dedicated storage room or area
    of the plant... If a dedicated storage room or area is not available, we recommend that you
    store raw materials and other ingredients that contain allergens below those that do not
    contain allergens to prevent allergen cross-contact in the event of a spill or leak."
  summary: |
    储存隔离的空间规则：含过敏原与不含过敏原的原料分库/分区存放；没有条件分区时，
    含过敏原者必须放在下层——这样一旦泄漏或洒落，只落在含过敏原一侧，
    不会从上往下污染无过敏原原料。这是"重力方向决定堆放次序"的可直接套用规则。
  tags: [principle, rule, storage, segregation, allergen]

- id: p4-13
  title: 散装运输必须验证前载信息与车辆清洁，有疑点即拒收
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 附录 11-1.F.1
  source_quote: |
    "Establish and implement measures to ensure that raw materials and other ingredients that
    are received in bulk or food not completely enclosed by a container... have been
    transported and are being transferred in a manner that does not result in allergen
    cross-contact. Examples of such measures are verification of tanker and/or railcar cleaning
    (e.g., hopper, boxcar, tanker, wash-tags), prior load information... You should reject the
    shipment if there is evidence that food has not been protected from allergen cross-contact."
  summary: |
    散装/罐车/火车皮运输的非完全密封食品：必须验证罐车/车皮清洗凭证（wash-tags）、
    获取前载货物信息、清洁消毒运输设备。一旦有证据表明食品在运输中未受保护、
    可能发生过敏原交叉接触，就应拒收整批。
  tags: [principle, bulk-transport, verification, reject, allergen]

- id: p4-15
  title: CGMP 失效虽无法定纠正要求，也应主动实施纠正/纠正措施
  type: principle
  source_chapter: 附录 11-1.A
  source_quote: |
    "Although the CGMPs do not specify that you take corrective actions if you identify a
    failure of your CGMP measures to prevent allergen cross-contact, we recommend that you
    implement corrections or corrective actions as necessary and appropriate to ensure that the
    CGMP measures that are part of your food allergen program are operating as intended."
  summary: |
    法规只对预防控制的失效要求纠正措施，CGMP 层面的失效没有强制条款——但 FDA
    建议照样做：如设备不目视清洁就重新清洁（correction）；程序防不住交叉接触就
    修订程序、再培训人员（corrective action）。原则：CGMP 是过敏原程序的地基，
    地基失效也不能放任。
  tags: [principle, cgmp, corrective-action, allergen]

- id: p4-16
  title: 颜色编码系统之间必须防混淆
  type: principle

  source_quote: |
    "If your plant uses a color-coding system for purposes other than identification of food

- id: p4-17
  title: 标签管理控制的边界：盖子不带过敏原相关信息时颜色不算控制
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Ch11 §11.12.4.1.1（脚注 39）
  source_quote: |
    "If the lid does not provide any label information (such as the product name, the
    ingredient statement, or a 'Contains' statement) applicable to the food allergen profile of
    the product, a color code for a lid (e.g., to complement the color code on the tub) would
    not be a label management control, because the wrong color of lid would not be relevant to
    the question of whether the product is misbranded under section 403(w) of the FD&C Act."
  summary: |
    判定规则：一项措施只有在"出错会导致 403(w) 假标"时才是标签管理控制。
    盖子上若印有品名等过敏原相关信息，盖子色环用错就是标签管理控制失效；
    若盖子没有任何过敏原相关信息，色环用错与假标无关，不构成控制项。
  tags: [principle, rule, label-management, classification-boundary]

- id: p4-20
  title: 生产未开始=correction 不记录；生产已开始=corrective action 必记录并评估产品
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Ch11 §11.13.3.3（Bakery B）
  source_quote: |
    "Corrections: If production has not begun, these activities are corrections and the
    Production Supervisor does not document the correction... Corrective actions: If production
    has begun, these activities are corrective actions and are documented... The Production
    Supervisor determines whether product may have been affected (i.e. whether product may have
    come in contact with peanut protein), and, if so, evaluates any affected cookies for safety
    and prevents affected food from entering commerce by re-processing... or diverting... to
    animal food."
  summary: |
    correction/corrective action 的时间分界规则在过敏原场景的应用：错色器具/服装
    在开工前发现——拿去清洗/更换即可，属 correction 且不必记录；开工后发现——
    属 corrective action，必须记录，并判定产品是否可能接触花生蛋白、评估安全性、
    以改做花生酱饼干或转动物饲料的方式阻止入市。
  tags: [principle, correction-vs-corrective-action, decision-rule, allergen]

- id: p4-21
  title: 花生严重性决定额外控制：即使产线隔离也要管控器具/服装/人员流动
  type: principle
  source_chapter: Ch11 §11.13.2.2（Bakery B）
  source_quote: |
    "Although Bakery B processes two different cookies (i.e., oatmeal cookies and caramel chip
    cookies) on the same production line, these cookies have the same food allergen profile.
    Therefore, Bakery B's PCQI determined that general cleaning measures... coupled with its
    segregation of processing lines, are adequate... However, due to the severity of allergic
    reactions in consumers who are allergic to peanuts, Bakery B's PCQI determined that
    allergen cross-contact controls on utensils, tools, apparel, and movement of personnel are
    necessary"
  summary: |
    同线同过敏原谱的产品，通用清洁+产线隔离即足够、无需设备卫生控制；
