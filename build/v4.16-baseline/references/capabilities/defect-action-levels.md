# 食品缺陷行动水平与原料验收判定（defect-action-levels）

*来源分层：本卡取用两类权威，引用前先分层。① **21 CFR Part 117 法规正文**（〔§ 117.110〕〔§ 117.80(b)(3)(4)〕〔§ 117.3〕，具法律约束力，标注〔§ 117.xxx〕）；② **FDA《Food Defect Levels Handbook》**（网页版，页面标注 Content current as of 09/07/2018，正文版本 May 1995 / Rev. 1997-03、1998-05 / 2005-02 更正），是 FDA 汇编的 defect action levels 一览与术语表，属**执法裁量参考**，标注「Handbook 章名」。它不是法规、不是 Guidance for Industry 意义上的 should 建议层、也不是检查员抽样作业指导；三类不可混引。*

*本卡定位：把「我们检出虫碎片 / 鼠毛 / 霉变，按什么标准判、能不能放行、能不能掺混」从查一个数字，变成**先分法源 → 再查表 → 再判义务 → 最后才谈放行**的判定问题。给的是判定路径与阈值出处，不是可用于签发放行单的检验作业指导。*

## R — 原文

**① 法定义务在 Subpart B，不在手册（21 CFR Part 117 法规正文）**：

> "(a) The manufacturer, processor, packer, and holder of food must at all times utilize quality control operations that reduce natural or unavoidable defects to the lowest level currently feasible. (b) The mixing of a food containing defects at levels that render that food adulterated with another lot of food is not permitted and renders the final food adulterated, regardless of the defect level of the final food. For examples of defect action levels that may render food adulterated, see the Defect Levels Handbook, which is accessible at http://www.fda.gov/pchfrule and at http://www.fda.gov."
> —— 〔§ 117.110 Defect action levels〕

> "*Defect action level* means a level of a non-hazardous, naturally occurring, unavoidable defect at which FDA may regard a food product 'adulterated' and subject to enforcement action under section 402(a)(3) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act."
> —— 〔§ 117.3〕定义

> "(3) Raw materials and other ingredients susceptible to contamination with aflatoxin or other natural toxins must comply with FDA regulations for poisonous or deleterious substances before these raw materials or other ingredients are incorporated into finished food. (4) Raw materials, other ingredients, and rework susceptible to contamination with pests, undesirable microorganisms, or extraneous material must comply with applicable FDA regulations for natural or unavoidable defects if a manufacturer wishes to use the materials in manufacturing food."
> —— 〔§ 117.80(b)(3)(4)〕

**② 手册自述的法律依据（注意：此处引用已过时）**：

> "Title 21, Code of Federal Regulations, Part 110.110 allows the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to establish maximum levels of natural or unavoidable defects in foods for human use that present no health hazard. These "Food Defect Action Levels" listed in this booklet are set on this premise--that they pose no inherent hazard to health."
> —— Handbook INTRODUCTION（原文照录；**v4.7 更正**：`21 CFR Part 110.110` 指向的是**至今仍有效**的 Part 110——FDA 2018-09-12 撤回了 2015 规则拟于 2018-09-17 删除 part 110 的 amendatory instruction 13；但 part 110 **正文本包未收**，须标「转引未收」。Part 117 侧对应条款为〔§ 117.110〕，详见 B2 与 CAP-20 B2）

**③ 手册自己划的四条边界**：

> "Poor manufacturing practices may result in enforcement action without regard to the action level. Likewise, the mixing of blending of food with a defect at or above the current defect action level with another lot of the same or another food is not permitted. That practice renders the final food unlawful regardless of the defect level of the finished food."
> —— Handbook INTRODUCTION

> "It is incorrect to assume that because the FDA has an established defect action level for a food commodity, the food manufacturer need only stay just below that level. The defect levels do not represent an average of the defects that occur in any of the products--the averages are actually much lower. The levels represent limits at which FDA will regard the food product "adulterated"; and subject to enforcement action under Section 402(a)(3) of the Food, Drug, and Cosmetics Act."
> —— Handbook INTRODUCTION

> "The FDA set these action levels because it is economically impractical to grow, harvest, or process raw products that are totally free of non-hazardous, naturally occurring, unavoidable defects. Products harmful to consumers are subject to regulatory action whether or not they exceed the action levels."
> —— Handbook INTRODUCTION

> "If there is no defect action level for a product, or when findings show levels or types of defects that do not appear to fit the action level criteria, FDA evaluates the samples and decides on a case-by-case basis. In this procedure, FDA's technical and regulatory experts in filth and extraneous materials use a variety of criteria, often in combination, in determining the significance and regulatory impact of the findings."
> —— Handbook PRODUCTS WITHOUT DEFECT LEVELS

**④ 版本、技术与工具定位**：

> "As technology improves, the FDA may review and change defect action levels on this list. Also, products may be added to the list. The FDA publishes these revisions as *Notices* in the *Federal Register*. It is the responsibility of the user of this booklet to stay current with any changes to this list."
> —— Handbook INTRODUCTION

> "It is FDA's position that pesticides are not the alternative to preventing food defects. The use of chemical substances to control insects, rodents and other natural contaminants has little, if any impact on natural and unavoidable defects in foods."
> —— Handbook USE OF CHEMICAL SUBSTANCES TO ELIMINATE DEFECT LEVELS

> "This edition of The Food Defect Action Level includes the source of each defect and the significance of it (i.e., how the defect affects the food). Food processors may find this information helpful as a quality control tool in their operation."
> —— Handbook USING THIS FOOD DEFECT ACTION LEVEL BOOKLET

**⑤ 术语：缺陷不必然是「安全危害」，但可以指示危害**：

> "Any foreign matter in a product associated with objectionable conditions or practices in production, storage, or distribution. Includes: objectionable matter contributed by insects, rodents, and birds; decomposed material; and miscellaneous matter such as sand, soil, glass, rust, or other foreign substances."
> —— Handbook GLOSSARY, EXTRANEOUS MATERIALS

> "Refers to the real or potential impact on the consumer due to the presence of a particular defect. A listed defect can have more than one significance to the consumer (e.g., the mold defect of whole cassia has an aesthetic significance, whereas the mold defect of green coffee beans has a potential health hazard significance due to the threat of mold toxins produced by the mold species known to infect coffee beans)."
> —— Handbook GLOSSARY, SIGNIFICANCE OF DEFECT

**⑥ 商品表的阈值实例（原文逐字，`source-defect-levels-handbook.md` COMMODITIES AND DEFECT ACTION LEVELS）**：

> "Allspice, Ground | Insect Filth (AOAC 981.21) | Average of 30 or more insect fragments per 10 grams" / "Allspice, Ground | Rodent filth (AOAC 981.21) | Average of 1 or more rodent hairs per 10 grams"
> "Apple Butter | Mold (AOAC 975.51) | Average of mold count is 12% or more" / "Apple Butter | Rodent filth (AOAC 945.76) | Average of 4 or more rodent hairs per 100 grams of apple butter"
> "Wheat Flour | Insect filth (AOAC 972.32) | Average of 75 or more insect fragments per 50 grams" / "Wheat Flour | Rodent filth (AOAC 972.32) | Average of 1 or more rodent hairs per 50 grams"
> "Allspice, Whole | — | DEFECT SOURCE: : Preharvest and/or post harvest infection SIGNIFICANCE: Potential health hazard - may contain mycotoxin producing fungi"

## I — 自述

这一卡处理的是**「非危害性的天然/不可避免缺陷」这一类特殊判定**：它既不是食品安全危害，又不是可以不管的质量问题，法定义务与执法裁量分置两处。七条结构化判定：

1. **先分法源，再谈数字**。法定义务只有一条主线：〔§ 117.110(a)〕`must ... reduce natural or unavoidable defects to the lowest level currently feasible`（**持续义务、结果义务**）＋〔§ 117.110(b)〕掺混稀释禁令＋〔§ 117.80(b)(3)(4)〕原料/返工品验收，执法钩子是 FD&C Act § 402(a)(3)（掺假）与 § 402(a)(4)（不卫生条件）。Handbook 是 FDA 汇编的**行动水平一览表**，§ 117.3 定义里写的是 `at which FDA may regard ... adulterated`——`may` 是 FDA 的执法裁量，不是企业的达标线。手册自述也只说 "Food processors may find this information helpful as a quality control tool"。**把 action level 当成「法规允许的上限」是本卡要纠正的第一错误**。
2. **action level 不是安全港、不是平均值、不是目标值**。手册原文连说三句：`It is incorrect to assume ... need only stay just below that level`、`do not represent an average ... the averages are actually much lower`、`Products harmful to consumers are subject to regulatory action whether or not they exceed the action levels`。所以：① 低于阈值也可能因卫生失守被判掺假（402(a)(4)）；② 把内部规格定在阈值下方一点点，仍可能违反 `lowest level currently feasible`；③ 检出了危害（如霉菌毒素），是否超过 action level 与是否要召回无关。
3. **`110.110` 不是过时的法规引用（v4.7 更正）**。网页 INTRODUCTION 首句写 `21 CFR Part 110.110`；§110.110(c) 那句 "Compliance with defect action levels does not excuse violation of ... 402(a)(4) ... or ... current good manufacturing practice" 正是手册 "Poor manufacturing practices may result in enforcement action without regard to the action level" 的出处。2015-09-17 PCHF 最终规则的 amendatory instruction 13（80 FR 56144）本拟于 2018-09-17 移除 part 110，但 **FDA 2018-09-12 最终规则（FR Doc. 2018-19855）撤回该指令**，Part 110 至今有效、eCFR 仍列 11 个 sections。所以对 `110.110` 的正确处理是「**现行仍有效，但正文本包未收 → 转引未收**」，不是「判为过时」。〔§ 117.110〕**只有 (a)(b) 两款**，没有复述 110.110(c) 的免责例外句；那句**不得标成〔§ 117.110(c)〕**，理由是它是 part 110 的 (c) 款，而非「part 110 已删」。Part 117 体系下该层约束来自 402(a)(4) 与 Subpart B 整体。本包给 Part 117 义务时一律写〔§ 117.110〕。
4. **措辞差异以法规为准**。手册说混合「at or above the current defect action level」不合法；〔§ 117.110(b)〕写的是「at levels that render that food adulterated」。后者更宽（不以上表列明的 action level 为唯一判据），且明确 `regardless of the defect level of the final food`——**终检合格救不了掺混稀释**。这与 `chemical-hazard-controls` 卡的「不得用掺混稀释处理超标批次」纪律是同一条；要区分的是〔§ 117.80(c)(9)〕允许经证明有效的返工 reconditioning。
5. **没有 action level ≠ 没有标准**。Handbook PRODUCTS WITHOUT DEFECT LEVELS 明说：无对应阈值、或检出的缺陷类型/水平不契合表内判据时，FDA 由 filth and extraneous materials 技术与法规专家**逐案评估**，考量毛发长度、虫碎片尺寸、污物在样品中的分布、多种 filth 组合，并结合物种生态学与产品种植/采收/加工知识。所以「表里没这个产品，所以合格」是错的；此时更要回到〔§ 117.110(a)〕的持续义务与卫生记录。
6. **美学缺陷与食品安全危害之间有一道桥，叫 SIGNIFICANCE**。表中多数条目标 `SIGNIFICANCE: Aesthetic`，但 Allspice Whole、Capsicum Pods、Ground Paprika、Cocoa Beans、Coffee Beans Green、Figs、Ginger Whole、Nutmeg Whole 等明确标 `Potential health hazard - may contain mycotoxin producing fungi`；GLOSSARY 的 SIGNIFICANCE OF DEFECT 还特意用 whole cassia（美学）与 green coffee beans（霉菌毒素）对照。**看到 mold / rot / decomposed 类缺陷，必须顺手联动危害分析**：〔§ 117.80(b)(3)〕要求易受黄曲霉毒素等天然毒素污染的原料先符合 FDA 关于有毒有害物质的规定，〔§ 117.130〕危害分析里该原料的化学危害不能因为「只是 aesthetic defect」被判 No。反之，也不要把 rodent hair / insect fragments 一律升格为生物危害。
7. **农药不能替代预防，版本会随技术演进**。Handbook 明确 `pesticides are not the alternative to preventing food defects`，且对天然不可避免缺陷 `little, if any impact`；靠加大田间用药来"达标"既不解决问题，还可能引入农残危害。手册版本是 May 1995、修订 1997-03 / 1998-05、2005-02 两处方法来源更正，页面标注 Content current as of 09/07/2018；修订以 **Federal Register Notices** 发布，`It is the responsibility of the user of this booklet to stay current`。引用任何具体数值前必须核对现行版本，不能把 1995 年的表当今天的定论。

## A1 — 书中案例

**案例 1：终检合格仍属掺假（〔§ 117.110(b)〕 / Handbook INTRODUCTION）**
某批香辛料虫碎片超出表列水平，操作员把它按比例掺进三批合格料，终产品复检全部"低于阈值"。法规原文 `regardless of the defect level of the final food` 直接否定这种做法：掺混行为本身使终产品 adulterated。SECG XIII.H 用白话复述同一条并指向本手册；`cgmp-compliance-guide` 卡案例 4 也是这条。**判据是行为（是否掺混）与来源批状态，不是终检数值**。

**案例 2：「我们一直控制在阈值以下」不构成合规证据（Handbook INTRODUCTION / 〔§ 117.110(a)〕）**
企业内控规格按 "Average of 30 or more insect fragments per 10 grams"（Allspice, Ground）倒推，定在 28 片/10 g 就算合格。手册明确阈值不是平均值、行业实际平均值远低于它，且法规要求的是 `lowest level currently feasible`。**正确做法是记录现行可行技术下的实际水平与持续改进证据**（原料分拣、筛分、供应商田间管理、仓储虫害防治的运行记录），而不是把执法裁量线当内控目标。

**案例 3：一个 aesthetic 标签下的潜在健康危害（Handbook COMMODITIES / GLOSSARY）**
Allspice, Whole 的 Mold 阈值是 `Average of 5% or more berries by weight are moldy`（MPM-V32），但同一条 DEFECT SOURCE 行的 SIGNIFICANCE 写的是 `Potential health hazard - may contain mycotoxin producing fungi`。若只按"美学缺陷"放行，就漏掉了〔§ 117.80(b)(3)〕的天然毒素前置要求与危害分析中的化学危害。**同一张表的 SIGNIFICANCE 列必须与缺陷类型一起读**，不能只看 ACTION LEVEL 一列。

**案例 4：表内没有这个产品（Handbook PRODUCTS WITHOUT DEFECT LEVELS）**
某复配调味料未列在 111 个产品里，检验报告称"FDA 无标准，判定合格"。手册明说此类由 FDA 专家逐案评估，考量 filth 类型组合、分布与加工史；〔§ 117.110(a)〕的持续义务也不因未列产品而消失。**无 action level 是「无预设阈值」，不是「无义务、无风险」**。

**案例 5：靠农药压缺陷（Handbook USE OF CHEMICAL SUBSTANCES）**
某坚果原料供应商为降低虫蛀率加大田间杀虫剂用量。FDA 立场是农药对天然/不可避免缺陷 `little, if any impact`，主要用途是保护作物免受毁灭性害虫与外观严重受损；此举既不满足 `lowest level currently feasible`，又可能新增农残化学危害。正确路径是采收/储运/加工环节的虫害预防与原料验收。

**案例 6：数值单位与方法绑定（Handbook USING THIS / COMMODITIES）**
Wheat Flour 是 `75 or more insect fragments per 50 grams`（AOAC 972.32），Allspice Ground 是 `30 or more ... per 10 grams`（AOAC 981.21），Apple Butter 是 `4 or more rodent hairs per 100 grams`（AOAC 945.76）。**每个数字都绑定取样量、基质与 AOAC/MPM 方法编号**，脱离方法谈"我们的检出值是 20，合格"没有意义；MPM 已绝版但网上可查，AOAC 方法正文本包未收。

10. 需要区分 defect action level（402(a)(3) 掺假裁量）与过敏原标签、微生物限值、污染物 action level 等其他阈值体系。

12. 引用 1995/2018 版手册数值前，需要核对是否已有 Federal Register Notice 修订或新增产品。

## E — 执行要点

**Step 1 — 先过法源关（不许跳）**。问三件事：① 这是法规义务还是 FDA 执法裁量？答案：Part 117 义务在〔§ 117.110〕〔§ 117.80(b)(3)(4)〕，裁量在 Handbook 表；② 引用的是 `110.110` 还是 `117.110`？前者**现行仍有效但正文未收**，标「转引未收」并回 Part 117 侧法条核对，不要判为过时（v4.7 更正）；③ 用户要的是'数值'还是'合规结论'？数值可给但必须附义务层说明，合规结论必须能回指〔§ 117.xxx〕。

**Step 2 — 定位产品与缺陷类型**。在 `source-defect-levels-handbook.md` 的 COMMODITIES AND DEFECT ACTION LEVELS 按产品英文名字母序查；同时读全该产品的三列（DEFECT + Method / ACTION LEVEL / DEFECT SOURCE + SIGNIFICANCE）。注意 111 个产品、173 条行动水平，说明行已从"缺陷"列移到 ACTION LEVEL 列并以 `—` 占位（转换说明见文件头）。

**Step 3 — 三道否定式核对**。① 低于阈值 ⇒ **不能**直接判合规，回 `lowest level currently feasible` 与 402(a)(4) 卫生条件；② 阈值 ⇒ **不是**平均值、不是内控目标；③ 表内无此产品 ⇒ **不是**无标准，按 PRODUCTS WITHOUT DEFECT LEVELS 的逐案评估逻辑处理。任一环节被跳过，结论作废。

**Step 4 — 缺陷类型分诊（安全 / 非安全）**。若为 mold / rot / decomposed 或 SIGNIFICANCE 含 `Potential health hazard`，转入危害分析：〔§ 117.80(b)(3)〕天然毒素前置合规、〔§ 117.130〕化学危害识别、必要时供应商验证；若为 insect fragments / rodent hair 且仅标 `Aesthetic`，留在质量与 CGMP 侧，按〔§ 117.110(a)〕持续改进，**不要一律升格为食品安全危害**。

**Step 5 — 不合格批处置**。掺混稀释一律禁止（〔§ 117.110(b)〕，且以法规 "at levels that render that food adulterated" 措辞为准）；区分经证明有效的 reconditioning 返工〔§ 117.80(c)(9)〕；记录 DEFECT SOURCE 指向的失控环节（preharvest → 供应商/田间，post harvest → 储运，processing → 厂内卫生与虫害防治），把纠正动作落到该环节。

**Step 6 — 版本与出处复核**。任何具体数值输出前：确认页面版本（Content current as of 09/07/2018）、检索是否有 FR Notice 修订，标明 AOAC / MPM 方法编号与取样量；结论里同时给出〔§ 117.xxx〕条款号。引用格式见 B8。

**Step 7 — 交叉路由**。cGMP 逐条取证 → `cgmp-compliance-guide`；掺混/天然毒素等化学危害控制 → `chemical-hazard-controls`；原料与供应商批准 → `supply-chain-verification`；危害分析判 No 的书面论证 → `hazard-analysis-method`；效力分层总口径 → `regulatory-boundaries`；参考资料使用纪律 → `reference-tools-discipline`。

## B — 边界

**B1 — 手册不是法规、不是安全港、不是放行标准。** 它是 FDA 汇编的行动水平一览与术语表，§ 117.3 定义用的是 `may regard ... adulterated`。合规结论必须回指〔§ 117.110(a)〕的 `lowest level currently feasible`；把 action level 写成"法规允许的最大值"或"FDA 要求必须低于"都是效力层错误。

**B2 — Part 110 至今有效；`110.110` 不是过时引用（v4.7 实质性纠错）。** Handbook 网页 INTRODUCTION 首句仍写 21 CFR Part 110.110。2015-09-17 PCHF 最终规则（80 FR 55908）的 **amendatory instruction 13**（56144 页）指示 Federal Register 于 2018-09-17 `remove and reserve part 110`；**FDA 2018-09-12 最终规则（FR Doc. 2018-19855，同日生效）撤回该指令**，官方措辞 `amendatory instruction 13 in the third column on page 56144 is withdrawn`，理由是部分小/极小微企业 part 117 合规日期延长至 2020-01-27，若删除会有 4–16 个月 CGMP 空档。`Retaining the Human Food CGMP regulation will maintain the status quo ... and will avoid an unintended gap in public health protection.`；FDA 并称将在所有企业达到 part 117 CGMP 合规日后另案移除。**eCFR 现行版 Part 110 仍列 11 个 sections（含 §110.110）**，§114.5 并列 `parts 110 and 117` 也是现行文本。由此：① 手册那句 "Poor manufacturing practices may result in enforcement action without regard to the action level" 是 **§110.110(c)** 的意译，**仍不得标成〔§ 117.110(c)〕**，但理由是「那是 part 110 的 (c) 款」，不再是「part 110 已删」；② Part 117 侧现行法条仍是〔§ 117.110〕（Subpart B，仅 (a)(b) 两款）＋〔§ 117.3〕定义＋〔§ 117.80(b)(3)(4)〕原料要求＋FD&C Act § 402(a)(3)/(4)，`lowest level currently feasible` 与掺混稀释禁令**结论不变**；③ part 110 正文本包未收，讨论时标「转引未收」；④ 2018-19855 正文属**外部核实、本包未收**。完整事实链见 CAP-20 B2。

**B3 — 不提供检验与抽样作业指导。** 表内数值绑定 AOAC / MPM 方法编号与规定取样量，本包**未收** AOAC Official Methods 与 MPM 方法正文（手册说明 MPM 已绝版、网上可查）。取样方案、制样、显微计数判读、实验室资质、样本量与判定不确定度均需回方法原文与有资质实验室；本卡不能替代检验 SOP，也不能据此签发放行单。

**B4 — 检查员视角已补四卡，法规正文层已扩为六套（v4.12 加 Part 108〔§ 108.xxx〕、v4.13 加 Part 120〔§ 120.xxx〕，见 CAP-25 / CAP-26），方法与制样仍缺。** 第七来源 CPGM 7303.040 现行版（`2025-CPM-HFP-001`）已收，见 CAP-16 `inspection-readiness`：给检查类型、取证深度、观察项分级与记录举证矩阵；**第八来源 CP 7303.050（2022 签发 / 2025-09-10 更正）已收**，见 CAP-17 `inspection-sampling`：给污秽/霉变抽样、产品样品量与 PART V 逐案执法阶梯；**第九来源 IOM 2026 Ch.5 已收**，见 CAP-18；**第十来源 CP 7303.070（2024 签发 / 2025-09-26 更正）已收**，见 CAP-19：给 LACF/AF 管辖分流、酸化判定三步、pH/aw 实验室判读与 Import Alert 升级；**第十一、十二来源 21 CFR Part 113 / 114 法规正文已收**，见 CAP-20 `lacf-statutory-requirements`：给企业法定义务与〔§ 117.1(c)〕〔§ 117.5(d)〕接口；**第十四来源 21 CFR Part 123（Fish and Fishery Products，4-1-25 Edition）已收**，见 CAP-22 `seafood-haccp-statutory-requirements`：给海产品 HACCP 义务、〔§ 117.5(b)〕双向锁定与与 FSVP 的分轨。本卡则给**阈值出处与义务边界**。**仍未收**：21 CFR part 108 / 110 / 120 正文（part 123 自 v4.9 起已收）、IOM 其余章节（尤其 Ch.4 的 702(b) 与制样）、RPM、FMD-86 / FMD-147、SOP-001052、CPG 全套、其他 Compliance Programs（7303.878 / 7303.080 / 7304.019 等）、Import Alerts 正文、AOAC / MPM 方法正文、21 CFR part 117 citations table 与 SOFO job aid；分析方法检出限、显微判读、证据充分性标准与警告信判定阈值须另行复核。

**B5 — 不覆盖其他阈值体系。** 本卡只讲"天然或不可避免、无健康危害的缺陷"的 defect action level。不要与以下混用：真菌毒素/污染物 action level 与容许量、微生物限值、过敏原阈值、农药残留容许量、L.mono 成品 `L. monocytogenes` 判定、食品接触材料迁移限量。它们法源、单位与后果都不同。

**B6 — 质量标准与食品安全危害的分界不可一刀切。** 全包 `out_of_scope` 声明不做"质量缺陷（虫害、毛发、腐败）等非食品安全问题的控制方案"，本卡提供的是**法定义务边界与阈值出处**，不是虫害防治/分拣/筛分的工程方案。但 SIGNIFICANCE 标 `Potential health hazard` 的霉菌类缺陷必须联动化学危害分析（见 I-6 / Step 4），此时已超出纯质量范畴。

**B7 — 版本与转换局限。** 页面标注 Content current as of 09/07/2018，正文版本 May 1995（Rev. 1997-03、1998-05；2005-02 两处方法来源更正 MPM-V92→MPM-V32、955.46→965.41），时间上早于或接近 Part 117 生效，故其法规引用脱节。`source-defect-levels-handbook.md` 由 mhtml 转出：rowspan 产品名向下重复展开、DEFECT SOURCE 说明行移至 ACTION LEVEL 列、嵌套的树坚果容许百分比表拆为独立小节、链接只留锚文本、`<br>` 压为空格、版面填充空行已删；英文正文措辞与数值未改动，检索以产品英文名与章名为准。

**B8 — 引用标注规范（全包统一，v4.7）。** 法规用〔§ 117.110〕〔§ 117.80(b)(3)〕〔§ 117.3〕，LACF/AF 法条另用〔§ 113.xxx〕〔§ 114.xxx〕；手册用「Handbook INTRODUCTION / PRODUCTS WITHOUT DEFECT LEVELS / GLOSSARY / COMMODITIES AND DEFECT ACTION LEVELS + 产品名」；SECG 用章号；PCHF 指南用 Ch/小节号；Ed.5 用问答编号；L.mono 指南用其章节号。**二十类不可混引**（CPGM 7303.040 用「PART x」、CP 7303.050 用「PART x / ATTACHMENT A Part x / Table x」、IOM 用「IOM 5.x.x」、CP 7303.070 用「PART x / ATTACHMENT x」标注），尤其不得把手册原文标成〔§ 117.xxx〕，不得把 § 117.110(b) 的措辞换成手册的 "at or above the current defect action level"，也不得把 §110.110(c) 标成〔§ 117.110(c)〕。

**B9 — 覆盖范围边界。** 本卡不做进口扣留应诉、警告信答复、行政文书代写、具体实验室检测或方法验证，也不做非美国体系（如中国 GB 2761 真菌毒素限量、GB 14881）的合规判定。用户任务超出能力卡路由范围时明确告知，不要硬套。
